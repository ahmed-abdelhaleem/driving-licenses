Some streets and areas have their own rules on top of the general ones. They are always marked with signs at the start – and usually an end sign.

## Pedestrian street and walking-speed area

[[signs:E7,E8,E9,E10]]

::: law tf 8:1
> På en gågata och i ett gångfartsområde gäller följande.
>
> 1. Fordon får inte föras med högre hastighet än gångfart.
> 2. Fordon får inte parkeras.
> 3. Fordonsförare har väjningsplikt mot gående.
>
> På en gågata får motordrivna fordon inte föras annat än för att korsa den. Sådana fordon får dock föras på gågator om det behövs för
>
> 1. varuleveranser till eller från butiker eller motsvarande vid gågatan,
> 2. transporter av gods eller boende till eller från adress vid gågatan,
> 3. transporter av gäster till eller från hotell eller motsvarande vid gågatan, eller
> 4. transporter av sjuka eller rörelsehindrade personer till eller från adress vid gågatan.

**In English:** "On a pedestrian street and in a walking-speed area the following applies: 1. Vehicles may not be driven faster than walking pace. 2. Vehicles may not be parked. 3. Drivers must give way to pedestrians. On a pedestrian street, motor vehicles may only be driven to cross it. However, they may be driven there when needed for 1. deliveries of goods to or from shops or similar on the street, 2. transport of goods or residents to or from an address on the street, 3. transport of guests to or from a hotel or similar on the street, or 4. transport of sick or disabled people to or from an address on the street."
:::

| | Pedestrian street ([[sign:E7|E7]]) | Walking-speed area ([[sign:E9|E9]]) |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Walking pace | Walking pace |
| Parking | Not allowed | Not allowed |
| Pedestrians | Drivers give way to them | Drivers give way to them |
| Through traffic with motor vehicles | **No** – only crossing, or the four exceptions | Allowed (at walking pace) |

A walking-speed area is designed so that pedestrians use the **whole surface** [[src:tf|10:8]] – so expect people, and children playing, anywhere. When you **leave** either one, you give way to the traffic on the road you enter [[src:tf|3:21]].

## Cycle street

[[signs:E33,E34]]

On a **cycle street** (*cykelgata*) [[src:tf|8:1a]]:

1. the speed limit is **30 km/h**,
2. you may only park in **specially arranged** parking places,
3. a driver who comes **onto** a cycle street from another road must **give way** to vehicles on it, and
4. drivers of motor vehicles must **adapt their speed to the cyclists**.

Leaving a cycle street, you give way to traffic on the road you enter [[src:tf|3:21]].

## Bus lanes

[[signs:D10,D11]]

A lane or carriageway for **vehicles in scheduled services** (*linjetrafik*) may only be used by those vehicles – and, if it is on the right-hand side, by bicycles and class II mopeds. A panel may allow other vehicles, such as taxis, or set times [[src:tf|8:2]] [[src:vmf|2:10]]. Other drivers may only **cross** the lane [[src:tf|3:10]] and may not stop or park in it [[src:tf|3:53]].

## Environmental zones

[[signs:E31-1,E31-2,E31-3,E32]]

An **environmental zone** (*miljözon*) is an area inside a built-up area where only vehicles with low enough emissions may be driven. There are three classes [[src:tf|4:22]]:

| Class | Applies to | Who may drive there (simplified) |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Heavy trucks and buses | Vehicles registered for the first time within the last six years, apart from some exceptions [[src:tf|4:22a]] |
| **2** | Cars, light trucks and light buses | **Petrol** (spark-ignition) vehicles meeting at least **Euro 5**; **diesel** vehicles meeting **Euro 6** [[src:tf|4:22b]] |
| **3** | Both groups | Mainly **electric**, **hydrogen fuel cell** and certain **gas** vehicles (for heavy vehicles also certain plug-in hybrids) [[src:tf|4:22c]] [[src:tf|4:22d]] |

In class 2 and 3 zones you must be able to show which emission standard your vehicle met, unless it is in the vehicle register [[src:tf|4:24a]]. The zone starts at sign E31 and ends at E32, the end of the built-up area, or another zone sign [[src:vmf|2:12]]. More about emissions in [[lesson:environment-and-vehicles]].

## Tunnels

[[signs:E26,E27,E28,E29-1]]

- You may **not stop or park** in a tunnel or underpass [[src:tf|3:53]].
- Sign [[sign:E26|E26]] gives the tunnel's length and is repeated every kilometre [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- In long tunnels you find **emergency lay-bys**, **emergency exits** and signs showing the way to them [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- Lane signals over the lanes are common in tunnels – a red cross means you must not use that lane [[src:vmf|3:15]].

## Toll roads and congestion tax

[[signs:E25]]

Sign [[sign:E25|E25]] tells you that a **fee or special tax** is charged for driving on a road or in an area [[src:vmf|2:12]] – for example a congestion tax.

## Fenced private areas

Inside a **fenced** railway, industrial or competition area or a similar fenced area, many of the traffic rules do not apply [[src:tf|1:2]]. But the law counts every road, street, square or other place that is **generally used for motor traffic** as a road [[src:vtdf|2]] – so on an ordinary car park or at a petrol station open to the public, the normal traffic rules apply.
