## Stopping or parking?

The law defines both words [[src:vtdf|2]] – see the full text in [[lesson:prohibitory-signs]]. In short:

| Situation | What it is |
|---|---|
| Standing still in a queue, at a red light or to avoid danger | **Neither** – it is part of driving |
| Letting someone get in or out, or loading or unloading goods | **Stopping** (*stannande*) |
| Everything else – also waiting in the car with the engine running | **Parking** (*parkering*) |

Where **parking** is forbidden, you may still **stop** briefly. Where **stopping** is forbidden, you may do neither.

## The two basic principles

Transportstyrelsen sums the rules up in two principles: you must not stop or park so that you **endanger** others, or **obstruct or inconvenience** others [[src:ts-parking]]. That comes straight from the law:

::: law tf 3:47
> Ett fordon får inte stannas eller parkeras
>
> 1. på en sådan plats eller på ett sådant sätt att fara uppstår eller trafiken onödigtvis hindras eller störs,
> 2. i en korsning med järnväg eller spårväg, eller
> 3. på ett sådant sätt att vägmärken eller trafiksignaler skyms.

**In English:** "A vehicle may not be stopped or parked 1. at such a place or in such a way that danger arises or traffic is unnecessarily obstructed or disturbed, 2. at a crossing with a railway or tramway, or 3. in such a way that road signs or traffic signals are hidden."
:::

## Where and how you stop or park

- Only on the **right** side of the road in your direction – on a **one-way** road also on the left, and on the left where there are rail tracks on the right [[src:tf|3:52]].
- **As far from the centre** of the road as possible, and **along** the road. Outside the carriageway if the road allows it [[src:tf|3:52]].
- Outside built-up areas, use a parking place on the **right** side if possible [[src:tf|3:52]].
- Not with any wheel **outside** a marked parking space, and not so that others cannot get to or drive away their vehicles [[src:tf|3:49]].
- In a **built-up area**, never on **public ground that is not road** – for example a lawn, a park or a ditch [[src:tf|3:48]].
- On a **footpath or cycle track**, only two-wheeled bicycles and two-wheeled class II mopeds may stop or park [[src:tf|3:48]].

## Never stop or park here

::: law tf 3:53
> Ett fordon får inte stannas eller parkeras
>
> 1. på eller inom ett avstånd av tio meter före ett övergångsställe, en cykelpassage eller en cykelöverfart,
> 2. i en vägkorsning eller inom ett avstånd av tio meter från en korsande körbanas närmaste ytterkant,
> 3. på eller inom ett avstånd av tio meter före en korsande cykelbana eller gångbana,
> 4. i en vägport eller tunnel,
> 5. på eller i närheten av ett backkrön eller i eller i närheten av en kurva där sikten är skymd,
> 6. längs en heldragen linje som anger gräns mellan körfält, om avståndet mellan fordonet och linjen är mindre än tre meter, såvida inte en streckad linje löper mellan fordonet och den heldragna linjen,
> 7. i ett cykelfält,
> 8. i ett spärrområde,
> 9. i en cirkulationsplats, eller
> 10. i ett körfält eller en körbana för fordon i linjetrafik m.fl.

**In English:** "A vehicle may not be stopped or parked 1. on, or within 10 metres before, a pedestrian crossing, a cycle passage or a cycle crossing, 2. in a junction or within 10 metres of the nearest edge of a crossing carriageway, 3. on, or within 10 metres before, a crossing cycle track or footpath, 4. in an underpass or tunnel, 5. on or near a hill crest, or in or near a bend where the view is blocked, 6. along a solid line between lanes if there are less than 3 metres between the vehicle and the line – unless a broken line runs between them, 7. in a cycle lane, 8. in a restricted (hatched) area, 9. in a roundabout, or 10. in a lane or carriageway for buses (vehicles in scheduled services)."
:::

Also never [[src:tf|3:47]] [[src:tf|9:1]]:

- **on a level crossing**,
- **on a motorway or expressway**, except at signed parking places and rest areas,
- where you **hide signs or signals**.

::: key Remember the number 10
**10 metres** before a pedestrian crossing, cycle passage, cycle crossing, crossing cycle track or footpath – and 10 metres from the edge of a crossing road in a junction. Ten metres is about two car lengths.
:::

## Never park here (stopping is allowed)

::: law tf 3:55
> Ett fordon får inte parkeras
>
> 1. på en väg inom ett avstånd av trettio meter från en plankorsning,
> 2. på en väg framför infarten till en fastighet eller så att fordonstrafik till eller från fastigheten väsentligen försvåras,
> 3. på en huvudled,
> 4. på en körbana bredvid ett annat fordon som stannats eller parkerats längs körbanans kant eller bredvid en anordning som har ställts upp där,
> 5. på en mötesplats som har märkts ut med märke E18, mötesplats, i vägmärkesförordningen (2007:90).

**In English:** "A vehicle may not be parked 1. on a road within 30 metres of a level crossing, 2. on a road in front of the entrance to a property, or so that vehicles going to or from the property are seriously hindered, 3. on a main road, 4. on a carriageway beside another vehicle that has stopped or parked along the edge, or beside an object placed there, 5. at a passing place marked with sign E18."
:::

Point 4 does not apply if the other vehicle is a two-wheeled bicycle, moped or motorcycle without a sidecar [[src:tf|3:55]].

Bus and tram stops: other vehicles may only stop there to let people in or out, without hindering the buses or trams. Without a marking on the road, this applies from **20 metres before to 5 metres after** the bus stop sign [[src:tf|3:54]]. The same kind of rule applies at special-purpose places, at places reserved for a vehicle type, and at **charging places** [[src:tf|3:54]].

You may not park on a **pedestrian street** or in a **walking-speed area**, and on a **cycle street** only in marked parking places [[src:tf|8:1]] [[src:tf|8:1a]].

## The 24-hour rule

::: law tf 3:49a
> På allmän plats inom tättbebyggt område där kommunen är huvudman för hållande av allmänna platser och inom vägområde för allmän väg får fordon parkeras högst 24 timmar i följd på vardagar, utom vardag före sön- och helgdag.

**In English:** "On public ground in a built-up area where the municipality is responsible for public places, and within the road area of a public road, vehicles may be parked for at most 24 hours in a row on weekdays, except a weekday before a Sunday or public holiday."
:::

## Fees, tickets and parking discs

Where conditions apply [[src:tf|3:49a]]:

- **Pay** as shown at the place, according to the tariff.
- Put a **parking ticket** at the **front** of the vehicle (or on it if that is not possible), easy to read from outside – even if parking is free.
- Set a **parking disc** to the **next half hour** after you parked. If you park before a time limit starts and will stay after it, set the time the limit begins. You do not need a disc if you only park when no time limit applies.

How to read the signs and panels is explained in [[lesson:additional-panels]].

## When you leave the vehicle

- Make sure it **cannot start rolling** – handbrake, and a gear or P position [[src:tf|3:50]].
- Make sure nobody else can **use** it – lock it and take the key [[src:tf|3:50]].
- Do not **open doors** or load and unload in a way that causes danger or unnecessary inconvenience. Look out for cyclists [[src:tf|3:50]].
- In darkness, dusk or dawn, a vehicle stopped or parked **on the road** must show its parking lights and rear lights, unless the road is well lit or the vehicle stands outside the carriageway and shoulder. On a road with a limit of 50 km/h or lower, the lights on the side towards the middle of the road are enough for a car up to 6 m long and 2 m wide [[src:tf|3:74]] [[src:tf|3:75]].

## If the vehicle breaks down

If an accident, engine failure or similar leaves your vehicle where stopping or parking is not allowed, you must have it moved to a suitable place **as soon as possible** [[src:tf|3:51]]. Where the limit is **above 50 km/h** and the vehicle stands on the carriageway or shoulder where it may not stop, you must put out a **warning triangle** [[src:tf|3:57]]. See [[lesson:accidents-first-aid]].

## Parking fines and removal

On public streets, the police and municipal parking attendants can issue a **parking fine** (*felparkeringsavgift*); the **vehicle owner** is responsible for paying it, even if they want to appeal. On private land, the landowner can set rules and charge a fee (*kontrollavgift*) if the rules are shown on signs [[src:ts-parking]]. A wrongly parked vehicle can also be **removed** – for example if it obstructs traffic or snow clearing, stands in a bus lane, on a footpath or cycle track, or within 5 metres before a pedestrian crossing, or has stood in the same place for at least **three days** [[src:ts-parking]].
