## What is a level crossing?

A **level crossing** (*plankorsning*) is a crossing **at the same level** between a road and a railway or a tramway that runs on its own embankment [[src:vtdf|2]]. Trafikverket's advice: trains are **quiet, fast** and have a **long braking distance**, and they **cannot swerve or stop suddenly** – so make it a habit to always **stop, look and listen** [[src:tv-level-crossings]]. Trains in principle always have priority over road traffic [[src:tf|2:5]].

## The signs before a crossing

[[signs:A35,A36,A37,A38-1,A38-2,A38-3,A39-1,A39-3]]

- [[sign:A35|A35]] – a railway crossing **with barriers** ahead.
- [[sign:A36|A36]] – a railway crossing **without barriers** ahead.
- [[sign:A37|A37]] – a **tramway** crossing without barriers ahead.
- [[sign:A38|A38]] – count-down markers: three stripes, two stripes, one stripe – each marks a third of the distance left [[src:vmf|2:5]].
- [[sign:A39|A39 St Andrew's cross]] – stands **immediately before** the crossing, normally at least 5 m before the nearest rail [[src:vmf|2:5]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|2:20]].

The warning signs A35–A37 are used outside built-up areas, and in built-up areas where the limit is above 50 km/h [[src:tsfs-2019-74|2:14]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|2:15]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|2:16]]. In towns you may meet a crossing with only the St Andrew's cross. Sometimes a **stop sign** ([[sign:B2|B2]]) stands at the crossing – then you must stop every time.

## Lights, bells and barriers

[[signs:Y1,Y2,Y3,Y4]]

- **[[sign:Y1|Y1 light signal]]** – two red lights flashing in turn, on a triangular screen with the point down: **stop** at the stop line or before the signal. A **white** flashing light below the red lights is there to make the crossing **easier to notice** [[src:vmf|6:6]].
- **[[sign:Y2|Y2 bell]]** – a ringing sound: **stop** [[src:vmf|6:6]].
- **[[sign:Y3|Y3 barrier]]** – **full barriers** close the whole road; **half barriers** close only the right-hand lane for traffic approaching the crossing [[src:tv-level-crossings]]. A lamp on the barrier flashes red from when it starts to go down until it is fully up again [[src:vmf|6:7]]. The back of the barrier may say *Kör genom bommen* – "drive through the barrier" [[src:vmf|6:8]].

The warning always **starts with flashing red lights and a bell** before the barriers come down [[src:tv-level-crossings]].
- **[[sign:Y4|Y4 marker board]]** – makes the crossing easier to see [[src:vmf|6:9]].

## When you may not drive onto the crossing

::: law tf 2:7
> En trafikant som har för avsikt att korsa en järnväg eller spårväg skall vara särskilt försiktig och vara uppmärksam på om något tåg eller någon spårvagn närmar sig. Förare av fordon skall anpassa hastigheten så att fordonet kan stannas före korsningen. Korsningen skall passeras utan onödigt dröjsmål.
>
> En trafikant får inte färdas in i en korsning med järnväg eller spårväg
>
> 1. om ett tåg eller en spårvagn närmar sig,
> 2. när en ljussignal visar rött sken, en ljudsignal ljuder eller en bom fälls, är fälld eller reses, eller
> 3. om det finns risk för att trafikanten måste stanna i korsningen.
>
> En trafikant som inte får färdas in i en korsning med järnväg eller spårväg skall stanna på betryggande avstånd från korsningen och före signaler eller bommar.

**In English:** "A road user who intends to cross a railway or tramway must be especially careful and watch for any train or tram approaching. Drivers must adapt their speed so the vehicle can be stopped before the crossing. The crossing must be passed without unnecessary delay. A road user may not enter a crossing with a railway or tramway 1. if a train or tram is approaching, 2. when a light signal shows red, a bell rings, or a barrier is going down, is down or is going up, or 3. if there is a risk of having to stop on the crossing. A road user who may not enter must stop at a safe distance from the crossing, before the signals or barriers."
:::

::: key Barriers going up = still wait
You may not drive on while the barrier is **going up** either – wait until it is fully up and the lights have stopped [[src:tf|2:7]]. With several tracks, a second train may come as soon as the first has passed. And never drive round barriers because they have been down "too long" [[src:tv-level-crossings]].
:::

::: warning The warning starts just as you reach the crossing?
Trafikverket's answer: **stop before the barriers if you can. If you cannot – drive on. Never stop on the track** [[src:tv-level-crossings]].
:::

::: steps
1. **Slow down** so you can stop before the crossing [[src:tf|2:7]].
2. **Look and listen** both ways – lower the volume, and open a window if you cannot see or hear well.
3. **Stop** at a safe distance if a train is coming, the lights flash, the bell rings or the barriers move.
4. Only drive on if there is **room on the other side** for your whole vehicle – never stop on the tracks [[src:tf|2:7]].
5. **Cross without delay.** Do not change gear on the crossing if you can avoid it.
:::

## Overtaking, stopping and parking

- **No overtaking** just before or on a level crossing – unless it has **barriers** or is controlled by the same kind of **traffic lights** as a road junction [[src:tf|3:39]].
- **No stopping or parking** on the crossing [[src:tf|3:47]].
- **No parking** within **30 metres** of a level crossing – both **before and after** it [[src:tf|3:55]] [[src:tv-level-crossings]].

## If your vehicle stops on the tracks

::: law tf 3:57
> Om ett fordon har blivit stående i en plankorsning och inte genast kan flyttas, ska föraren även vidta de åtgärder som behövs för fordonspassagerarnas säkerhet och för att varna förare av järnvägståg eller spårvagn.

**In English:** "If a vehicle has come to a stop on a level crossing and cannot be moved at once, the driver must also take the measures needed for the safety of the passengers and to warn the drivers of trains or trams."
:::

::: steps
1. **Get everyone out at once** and well away from the tracks.
2. **Call 112** (or use the 112 app, which shows your position) [[src:tv-level-crossings]].
3. Each of Trafikverket's level crossings has a **five-digit ID number**. It is written, with Trafikverket's phone number, on the **back of the St Andrew's cross** on the right-hand side before the crossing [[src:tv-level-crossings]]. Giving the ID helps them locate the crossing.
4. Only if there is time and it is clearly safe: try to move the vehicle off the tracks.
:::

If you are trapped **between closed barriers**, remember the text that may be on the back of the barrier: *Kör genom bommen* – drive through the barrier [[src:vmf|6:8]].
