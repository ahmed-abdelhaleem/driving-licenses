## The weights you need to understand

| Swedish | English | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *tjänstevikt* | **kerb weight** | The vehicle ready to drive – fuel, oil, water, tools, spare wheel and, for a car, the **driver** [[src:vtdl|2]]. |
| *totalvikt* | **total weight** (maximum authorised mass) | Kerb weight plus the **largest number of passengers and the most goods** the vehicle is built for. It is in the registration certificate [[src:vtdl|2]]. |
| *maxlast* / *maximilast* | **maximum load** | The most passengers and goods you may load: total weight minus kerb weight [[src:ts-weights]]. |
| *bruttovikt* | **gross weight** | What the vehicle **actually weighs at the moment**, with everything in it [[src:vtdl|2]]. |
| *släpvagnsvikt* | **trailer weight** | A trailer's own weight plus its load [[src:vtdl|2]]. |

::: key Licence rules use total weight
Whether your **licence** covers a combination depends on the **total weights** in the registration certificates – not on how much is actually loaded. An empty trailer with a high total weight counts with its total weight.
:::

See also [[term:tjanstevikt]], [[term:totalvikt]], [[term:maxlast]] and [[term:bruttovikt]] in the dictionary.

## What a B licence covers

::: law kkl 2:5
> B 1. personbil med totalvikt av högst 3,5 ton och lätt lastbil samt enbart ett lätt släpfordon som är kopplat till en sådan bil.

**In English:** "B: 1. a passenger car with a total weight of at most 3.5 tonnes and a light truck, together with only one light trailer connected to such a car."
:::

And a **light trailer** (*lätt släpfordon*) is defined as [[src:vtdl|2]]:

1. a trailer with a **total weight of at most 750 kg**, **or**
2. a trailer with a total weight **over 750 kg**, provided that the **car's and the trailer's total weights together** are **at most 3,500 kg**.

::: example Can I tow it with B?
- Car total weight **1,900 kg** + trailer total weight **750 kg** → yes (trailer is at most 750 kg).
- Car total weight **2,000 kg** + trailer total weight **1,300 kg** = 3,300 kg → **yes** (together at most 3,500 kg).
- Car total weight **2,400 kg** + trailer total weight **1,300 kg** = 3,700 kg → **no**, not with plain B. You need B96 or BE.
:::

## B96 and BE

- **B96** (extended B, *utökad behörighet B*) – you have passed a **special driving test** and may tow a trailer over 750 kg where the combined total weight is **over 3,500 kg but at most [[fact:licence.b96Max]]** [[src:kkl|2:7a]] [[src:ts-vehicles]].
- **BE** – a B car with one or more trailers, where the **trailers'** combined total weight is at most **3,500 kg** [[src:kkl|2:5]].

## Rules for the car as well

Your licence is only one part. The **car** must also be allowed to tow the trailer. Everything is in the car's **registration certificate, part 1** [[src:ts-trailer-weights]]:

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **O.1** | Maximum weight of a **braked** trailer (its actual weight with load) |
| **O.2** | Maximum weight of an **unbraked** trailer |
| **F.3** | Maximum **combined** gross weight of car and trailer (*tågvikt*) |
| **(34)** | The highest trailer **total weight** allowed with a **B** licence and with **B96** |

If the trailer fields are empty, the car is not approved for towing [[src:ts-trailer-weights]]. And even if your licence allows a trailer, it may never be loaded so that its actual weight exceeds the car's maximum trailer weight [[src:ts-trailer-weights]].

- The **maximum trailer weight** for the car may not be exceeded [[src:tf|4:3]].
- The **total weight** of the car and of the trailer may never be exceeded [[src:tf|4:4]].
- A motor vehicle may tow at most **one** vehicle not made for coupling, or **two** made for coupling, and the towed vehicles' combined gross weight may not exceed the towing vehicle's gross weight on roads – unless the towed vehicles have effective brakes [[src:tf|4:2]].
- The trailer must be coupled in a **safe** way [[src:tf|4:5]].

## Speed limits with a trailer

| Combination | Max speed |
|---|---|
| Car with a trailer with effective brakes worked by the car's service brake, or with a rigid drawbar, total weight at most 3.5 tonnes and an overrun brake (a typical braked caravan or car trailer) | **[[fact:speed.trailer]]** |
| Car with a trailer whose total weight (or empty kerb weight) is at most **half the car's kerb weight, and at most 750 kg** | **80 km/h** |
| Other trailers whose actual weight is at most the car's actual weight | **40 km/h** |
| All other cases – including **towing a car** with a rope or bar | **30 km/h** |

All from the Road Traffic Ordinance [[src:tf|4:20]]. A lower limit on the road always applies first.

## Driving with a trailer

- The combination is **longer and heavier**: braking distance grows, acceleration is slower, and overtaking takes much longer.
- Take bends and junctions **wider** – the trailer cuts the corner.
- **Side wind** and passing trucks can make the trailer sway. Slow down gently; do not brake hard or steer sharply.
- **Reversing** with a trailer: turn the steering wheel the opposite way to where you want the trailer to go, in small movements.
- Before you drive, check the **coupling**, the **safety cable or chain**, the **lights** and the **tyres** of the trailer.

## Towing a broken-down car

- At most **30 km/h** [[src:tf|4:20]].
- If the towed car is not made for coupling (a car on a tow rope or bar) and the distance between the vehicles is more than **2.0 metres**, the tow rope or bar must be clearly **marked** – for example with a flag [[src:tf|4:5]].
- Someone must sit in the towed car to **steer and brake**.
- If the towed car's lights do not work, mark it as a load: in darkness a white light at the front and a red light at the back, with reflectors [[src:tf|3:68]] [[src:tf|3:82]].
- Because a car on a tow rope or bar may go at most 30 km/h, the combination may not use a **motorway or expressway** – only vehicles allowed to go at least 40 km/h may be driven there [[src:tf|9:1]] [[src:tf|9:2]].
