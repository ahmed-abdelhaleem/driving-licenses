## Seat belts

::: law tf 4:10
> Alla som färdas i ett fordon som är utrustat med bilbälten ska använda en plats som är försedd med bilbälte, om en sådan plats är tillgänglig, och använda bältet. Barn som är kortare än 135 centimeter ska använda bilbarnstol, bälteskudde eller annan särskild skyddsanordning för barn i stället för eller tillsammans med bilbältet.

**In English:** "Everyone travelling in a vehicle fitted with seat belts must use a seat with a seat belt, if one is available, and use the belt. Children shorter than 135 centimetres must use a child seat, booster cushion or other special child restraint instead of, or together with, the seat belt."
:::

This applies in cars, light and heavy trucks, cars converted to tractors, and three- and four-wheeled motorcycles and mopeds with a body [[src:tf|4:10]]. The belt does **not** have to be used when the vehicle is **not moving**, when **reversing**, or when driving **inside a car park, multi-storey car park, petrol station or workshop area** or similar [[src:tf|4:10d]].

::: key Who is responsible?
Every passenger aged 15 or over is responsible for wearing their own belt. The **driver** must make sure that passengers **under 15** use a seat belt or child restraint [[src:tf|4:10c]].
:::

## Children in the car

### What the law says

1. Children **shorter than 135 cm** must use a **child seat, booster seat or booster cushion** (or other special child restraint) [[src:tf|4:10]].
2. A child **aged three or older** and shorter than 135 cm may not travel where a child restraint cannot be used – except for **occasional short trips**. Then the child uses the seat belt if possible, and may **not** sit in the **front seat** of a car or light truck [[src:tf|4:10]].
3. A child **under three** may not travel where a child restraint cannot be used – the only exception is an occasional short trip by **taxi**, and then not in the front seat [[src:tf|4:10]].

### What Transportstyrelsen advises

- Children are safest **rear-facing** until about **four years** old. Small children in forward-facing seats run **five times** the risk of being killed or seriously injured compared with rear-facing [[src:ts-children]].
- **Never** put a child in a baby seat, child seat, booster seat or booster cushion on a seat with an **active passenger airbag** – it can be deadly. A child is not safe in front of an active airbag until they are about **140 cm** tall. Side airbags are no danger to children in child seats [[src:ts-children]].
- Doctors and researchers recommend a booster seat or cushion until the child is **10–12 years** old [[src:ts-children]].

## How many passengers?

- A car may not carry more passengers than it is **registered** for, and passengers must sit on places **meant for passengers** [[src:tf|4:6]].
- Passengers may never be carried in a number or position that can cause danger [[src:tf|3:79]].
- Nobody may get on or off a moving vehicle, or ride on its steps [[src:tf|3:3]].

## Motorcycles and mopeds

::: law tf 4:6b
> På en motorcykel får passagerare tas med endast om motorcykeln är inrättad för det. På motorcykeln får högst en passagerare tas med. … Passagerare får tas med endast om föraren fyllt 18 år.

**In English:** "A passenger may only be carried on a motorcycle if the motorcycle is built for it. At most one passenger may be carried on the motorcycle. … A passenger may only be carried if the rider is at least 18 years old."
:::

- **Helmet:** everyone on a motorcycle or moped must wear a protective helmet. Exceptions include a passenger **under seven** who uses another suitable head protection, when the vehicle is **not moving**, and within car parks and petrol station areas [[src:tf|4:9]].
- The rider must make sure that passengers **under 15** wear a helmet [[src:tf|4:9]].
- No passengers may ride in a **trailer** behind a motorcycle [[src:tf|4:15b]].

::: only motorcycle
**Carrying a passenger** changes how the motorcycle behaves: longer braking distance, slower acceleration and different handling in bends. The passenger should hold on, keep their feet on the footrests, lean with you and not move about. Tyre pressure and suspension may need adjusting – see the owner's manual.
:::

## Loads

::: law tf 3:80
> Last får inte medföras på eller i ett fordon på sådant sätt att den kan
>
> 1. utgöra fara för person,
> 2. orsaka skador på egendom,
> 3. släpa efter eller falla av fordonet,
> 4. orsaka störande dammbildning eller liknande,
> 5. försvåra körningen av fordonet, eller
> 6. framkalla onödigt buller.
>
> Lasten skall vara fastgjord om det krävs med hänsyn till lastutrymmets eller lastens beskaffenhet. Om det behövs skall lasten vara övertäckt.

**In English:** "A load may not be carried on or in a vehicle in a way that can 1. be a danger to people, 2. damage property, 3. drag behind or fall off the vehicle, 4. cause disturbing dust or similar, 5. make the vehicle harder to drive, or 6. cause unnecessary noise. The load must be secured if the load space or the load requires it, and covered if needed."
:::

- A load may not block your **view**, make the vehicle hard to **manoeuvre**, or hide **indicators, brake lights, lamps or headlights** [[src:tf|3:78]].
- Straps, ropes and covers may not **hang loose** or drag on the ground [[src:tf|3:81]].
- A load that sticks out **in front**, or **more than 1 metre behind**, must be clearly **marked**. In darkness, dusk, dawn or bad weather it must be marked at the front with a **white light and white reflectors**, and at the back with a **red light and red reflectors** [[src:tf|3:82]].
- A load that sticks out more than **20 cm to the side**, or is more than 260 cm wide, must also be clearly marked [[src:tf|3:83]].
- The vehicle's **total weight** may never be exceeded – people and luggage count [[src:tf|4:4]]. Check the registration certificate.

::: tip Loose things in the car are dangerous
In a crash, a loose bag or bottle is thrown forward with many times its own weight. Put heavy things in the boot, low and against the back seat, and use the seat belts on empty seats to hold luggage if needed.
:::
