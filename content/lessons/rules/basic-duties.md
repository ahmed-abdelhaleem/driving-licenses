## The duty of care

Every traffic rule builds on one basic rule:

::: law tf 2:1
> För att undvika trafikolyckor skall en trafikant iaktta den omsorg och varsamhet som krävs med hänsyn till omständigheterna. Trafikanten skall visa särskild hänsyn mot barn, äldre, skolpatruller och personer som det framgår har ett funktionshinder eller en sjukdom som är till hinder för dem i trafiken.
>
> En trafikant skall uppträda så att han eller hon inte i onödan hindrar eller stör annan trafik.
>
> En vägtrafikant skall visa hänsyn mot dem som bor eller uppehåller sig vid vägen.

**In English:** "To avoid traffic accidents, a road user must take the care and caution that the circumstances require. The road user must show special consideration for children, older people, school crossing patrols and people who clearly have a disability or illness that makes traffic difficult for them. A road user must behave so as not to hinder or disturb other traffic unnecessarily. A road user must show consideration for those who live or spend time near the road."
:::

This rule applies **even when no specific rule fits**. Having right of way never removes your duty to do what you can to avoid a collision. In the theory test, the safest answer is often the one that shows care for others.

::: key Who needs extra care?
- **Children** – they are small, hard to see and can act suddenly.
- **Older people** – they may see, hear or move more slowly.
- **School crossing patrols** (*skolpatruller*).
- **People with a disability or illness** – for example a person with a white cane (impaired sight) or in a wheelchair.
:::

## Be fit to drive

::: law tf 3:1
> Fordon får inte föras av den som på grund av sjukdom, uttröttning, påverkan av alkohol, andra stimulerande eller bedövande ämnen eller av andra skäl inte kan föra fordonet på ett betryggande sätt.

**In English:** "A vehicle may not be driven by anyone who, because of illness, tiredness, the influence of alcohol or other stimulating or sedating substances, or for other reasons, cannot drive the vehicle safely."
:::

This rule applies **whatever the limits say**. Even below the alcohol limit, or after a legal medicine, you may not drive if you cannot drive safely. See [[lesson:alcohol-and-drugs]] and [[lesson:fatigue]].

## Keep your distance

::: law tf 3:2
> Avståndet till ett framförvarande fordon skall anpassas så att det inte finns risk för påkörning om det saktar in eller stannar. Avståndet skall också anpassas så att andra trafikanters omkörning underlättas.

**In English:** "The distance to a vehicle in front must be adapted so that there is no risk of running into it if it slows down or stops. The distance must also be adapted so that it is easier for others to overtake."
:::

The law gives **no number** – your distance must be long enough for the **speed and conditions**. A common rule of thumb (not a law) is to stay at least **3 seconds** behind in good conditions, and much more on wet or icy roads. [[lesson:speed-and-stopping-distance]] explains why.

When you drive behind a slow vehicle on a road where others want to overtake, leave a **gap** so that an overtaking car can pull in between you.

## Give free way to emergency vehicles and trains

::: law tf 2:5
> En trafikant skall lämna fri väg för
>
> 1. utryckningsfordon som avger signal med föreskriven larmanordning, och
> 2. järnvägståg eller spårvagn om inte spårvagnsföraren har väjningsplikt som utmärks genom vägmärke.
>
> Förare som skall lämna fri väg skall stanna om det är nödvändigt.

**In English:** "A road user must make way for 1. an emergency vehicle giving a signal with its prescribed alarm device, and 2. a train or tram, unless the tram driver has to give way as shown by a road sign. A driver who must make way must stop if necessary."
:::

An [[term:utryckningsfordon|emergency vehicle]] (ambulance, fire engine, police) with **siren and blue lights** must get through. What to do:

::: steps
1. **Stay calm and look** – where is it coming from, and where does it want to go?
2. **Make room** – usually by moving as far to the **right** as you safely can and slowing down.
3. **Stop if necessary** [[src:tf|2:5]] – but try not to stop where you block its way, for example in the middle of a junction.
4. **Do not follow** close behind the emergency vehicle through queues or junctions.
5. When it has passed, check for **more emergency vehicles** before you drive on.
:::

::: warning Making room must not create new danger
Do what you **safely** can. A sudden swerve or hard braking can cause a collision with the traffic around you – the duty of care still applies [[src:tf|2:1]].
:::

## Do not break into a column

You may not break into or in any other way obstruct a **military column**, a column of **rescue service** vehicles, a **group of children** led by an adult, a **funeral procession** or any other procession [[src:tf|2:6]].

## Signal clearly – and in good time

::: law tf 3:65
> När en förare avser att starta från en vägkant eller att vända, svänga, byta körfält eller ändra fordonets placering i sidled på något annat inte oväsentligt sätt skall föraren ge tecken med körriktningsvisaren för att visa sin avsikt. …
>
> Tecken skall ges i god tid innan den avsedda manövern genomförs och vara väl synligt och otvetydigt. Teckengivningen skall upphöra så snart manövern är avslutad.

**In English:** "When a driver intends to start from the roadside, turn around, turn, change lanes or otherwise move the vehicle sideways in a way that is not insignificant, the driver must signal the intention with the direction indicator. … The signal must be given in good time before the manoeuvre, be clearly visible and unambiguous, and stop as soon as the manoeuvre is finished."
:::

- When you intend to **stop or slow down quickly**, your brake lights show it [[src:tf|3:65]].
- **Signalling does not give you the right to go.** You must still make sure the manoeuvre can be done without danger or unnecessary obstruction [[src:tf|3:66]].
- Use the **horn or headlight flash** only when needed to **prevent danger**, and not longer than necessary [[src:tf|3:64]].

## Things you must never do

- **Never move or change** road signs, markings, signals or other traffic devices. If you have knocked one over, put it back, or tell the police or the road authority at once [[src:tf|2:9]].
- **Never throw or leave** anything on the road that can cause danger. If something from your vehicle ends up on the road, remove it – or warn others until it is removed [[src:tf|2:10]].
- **No unnecessary noise**: do not let a motor vehicle make more noise or exhaust fumes than necessary, and do not drive around needlessly near homes [[src:tf|4:7]] [[src:tf|4:8]].
- **No needless slow driving or sudden braking** that hinders others without good reason [[src:tf|3:16]].

## The owner's responsibility

The **owner** of a vehicle must make sure it is not used in breach of the traffic rules. When someone else drives it, the owner is still responsible for some things – for example that the vehicle has winter tyres when they are required and is not overloaded [[src:tf|1:5]].

## Carry your licence

When you drive, you must carry your **driving licence** and show it if a police officer asks for it [[src:kkl|3:15]]. See [[lesson:after-the-test]].
