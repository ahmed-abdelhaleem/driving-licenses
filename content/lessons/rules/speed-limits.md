## The rule above all limits

::: law tf 3:14
> Ett fordons hastighet skall anpassas till vad trafiksäkerheten kräver. Hänsyn skall tas till väg-, terräng-, väderleks- och siktförhållandena, fordonets skick och belastning samt trafikförhållandena i övrigt. Hastigheten får aldrig vara högre än att föraren behåller kontrollen över fordonet och kan stanna det på den del av den framförvarande vägen eller terrängen som han eller hon kan överblicka och framför varje hinder som går att förutse.

**In English:** "A vehicle's speed must be adapted to what traffic safety requires. Account must be taken of the road, terrain, weather and visibility conditions, the condition and load of the vehicle, and the traffic conditions in general. The speed may never be so high that the driver loses control of the vehicle or cannot stop it within the part of the road or terrain ahead that he or she can see, and before every obstacle that can be foreseen."
:::

::: key The speed limit is a maximum, not a target
The limit tells you the **highest** speed allowed in **good** conditions. You must always be able to **stop within the distance you can see** to be clear. In fog, darkness, rain, snow or heavy traffic, the right speed is often well below the limit.
:::

## Fifteen situations that demand a low speed

On top of the general rule, the law lists situations where you must keep a speed that is **low enough for the circumstances** [[src:tf|3:15]]:

::: steps
1. in a **built-up area**,
2. when **visibility is reduced** by light or weather,
3. at **pedestrian crossings** and other places where people cross,
4. where **crossing traffic** may come,
5. in **sharp bends**,
6. at **hill crests** and other places where the view is blocked,
7. when there is a risk of **dazzle**,
8. when **meeting** other vehicles on **narrow roads**,
9. on **slippery** roads,
10. when approaching a **tram, bus or school bus** that has stopped to let passengers on or off,
11. when approaching **children** on or beside the road,
12. when approaching **animals** (livestock) on the road,
13. where **road works** are going on,
14. when passing the scene of an **accident**,
15. on **dirty roads** where others risk being splashed.
:::

You must also not drive **unnecessarily slowly** or brake suddenly without good reason, so that you hinder others [[src:tf|3:16]].

## The Swedish limit system

::: law tf 3:17
> Inom tättbebyggt område får fordon inte föras med högre hastighet än 50 kilometer i timmen. …
>
> Utom tättbebyggt område får fordon inte föras med högre hastighet än 70 kilometer i timmen (bashastighet).

**In English:** "Within a built-up area, vehicles may not be driven faster than 50 kilometres per hour. … Outside a built-up area, vehicles may not be driven faster than 70 kilometres per hour (the base speed)."
:::

| Where | Limit when no sign says otherwise | Can be changed to |
|---|---|---|
| **Built-up area** (after sign [[sign:E5|E5]]) | **[[fact:speed.builtUp]]** | 30 or 40 km/h by the municipality [[src:tf|3:17]] |
| **Outside built-up areas** | **[[fact:speed.outsideBuiltUp]]** – the [[term:bashastighet|base speed]] | 80, 90, 100 or 110 km/h by Trafikverket [[src:tf|3:17]] |
| **Motorway** (after sign [[sign:E1|E1]]) | **[[fact:speed.motorway]]** | 120 km/h by Trafikverket [[src:tf|9:1]] |

Lower limits can also be set by **local traffic regulations** where safety, traffic flow or the environment calls for it [[src:tf|10:1]]. All limits other than the default ones are shown with [[sign:C31-5|C31 speed limit signs]]. An **expressway** has no default limit of its own – follow the signs, or the base speed of 70 km/h.

[[signs:C31-4,C31-5,C31-7,C31-9,C31-11,C31-12]]

::: tip Reading the signs
- A C31 sign applies until the **next** C31 sign, the end of a temporary limit ([[sign:C32|C32]]), a pedestrian street ([[sign:E7|E7]]) or a walking-speed area ([[sign:E9|E9]]) – not just to the next junction [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- On a **pedestrian street** and in a **walking-speed area**, the limit is **walking pace** [[src:tf|8:1]]. On a **cycle street** ([[sign:E33|E33]]) it is **30 km/h** [[src:tf|8:1a]].
- **Recommended** speeds ([[sign:E11|E11]], [[sign:E13|E13]]) are not limits, but they tell you the conditions call for less speed. See [[lesson:information-signs]].
:::

## Special limits for some vehicles

Some vehicles have their own maximum speed, whatever the sign says. If the road has a **lower** limit, the lower limit applies [[src:tf|4:20]].

::: law tf 4:20
> Om det till ett motorfordon … har kopplats ytterligare fordon, får fordonen på väg inte föras med högre hastighet än som anges nedan.

**In English:** "If another vehicle has been connected to a motor vehicle …, the vehicles may not be driven on a road faster than shown below."
:::

| Vehicle combination | Maximum speed |
|---|---|
| Car with a trailer that has **effective brakes worked by the car's service brake**, or has a rigid drawbar, a total weight of at most 3.5 tonnes and an **overrun brake** (*påskjutsbroms*) | **[[fact:speed.trailer]]** |
| Car with a trailer whose total weight (or, when it is empty, its kerb weight) is **at most half the car's kerb weight, and at most 750 kg** – typically a small trailer without brakes | **80 km/h** |
| **Motorcycle** with a trailer | **80 km/h** |
| A trailer in other cases, if its **actual weight is not more than the towing vehicle's** | **40 km/h** |
| **Towing another vehicle** with a rope or tow bar, and all other cases | **30 km/h** |

These are from the Road Traffic Ordinance [[src:tf|4:20]]. Other examples from the same section: **heavy trucks** 80 km/h (90 km/h on motorways and expressways), and **class I mopeds** 45 km/h.

::: only car
**Towing and trailers** are covered in detail in [[lesson:towing-trailers]] – including which trailers your B licence allows.
:::

::: only motorcycle
**For riders:** a two-wheeled motorcycle may only tow a trailer that is at most **125 cm wide** and weighs at most **half the motorcycle's kerb weight** (not counting fuel and tools). You may never carry passengers in a trailer behind a motorcycle [[src:tf|4:15a]] [[src:tf|4:15b]].
:::

## Speed and the law

Speeding is punished with fines, and serious speeding can lead to the licence being **revoked** – during the **probation** period it means taking the tests again. See [[lesson:consequences]].
