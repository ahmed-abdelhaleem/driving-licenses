## Work through it in order

When you come to a junction, ask these questions **in this order** – the first one that gives an answer decides [[src:tf|2:2]] [[src:tf|2:3]]:

::: steps
1. **Is there a police officer** directing traffic? Follow the officer.
2. **Are there working traffic lights?** Follow the lights.
3. **Are there signs or markings** – give way, stop, main road? Follow them. See [[lesson:priority-signs]].
4. **Am I coming from a place where I always give way** – a car park, a property, a path, the shoulder? Then give way to everyone (see below).
5. **None of the above?** Then the **right-hand rule** applies.
:::

And whatever the answer: **give way** clearly by slowing down in good time, and drive on only when it causes no danger or obstruction [[src:tf|3:5]].

## The right-hand rule

::: law tf 3:18
> När två fordons kurser skär varandra har förare väjningsplikt mot det fordon som närmar sig från höger utom i de fall som avses i 21-23 §§.

**In English:** "When the paths of two vehicles cross, a driver must give way to the vehicle approaching from the right, except in the cases covered by sections 21–23."
:::

The [[term:hogerregeln|right-hand rule]] (*högerregeln*) applies in junctions **without** signs, signals or other rules – common in residential areas. Sign [[sign:A28|A28]] can warn of such a junction.

::: example Who goes first?
You come to a junction with no signs. A car approaches from your **right**, another from your **left**.
- You give way to the car from the **right**.
- The car on your **left** gives way to **you**.
- The car from the right drives first, then you, then the car from the left.

If four cars arrive at the same time from all four directions, nobody has a car on their left – so nobody can go by the rule alone. Then the drivers must **agree** – carefully, with eye contact and clear signals.
:::

::: warning The right-hand rule is not a right to go
Bicycles are vehicles too: a cyclist coming from your right in an unsigned junction has priority over you. And if the driver from your left does not give way, you must still avoid a collision [[src:tf|2:1]].
:::

## When you must give way to everyone

The right-hand rule does **not** apply in these cases – here you give way to **every vehicle whose path crosses yours** when you come onto a road [[src:tf|3:21]]:

- from a **car park**, a **property** (for example a driveway), a **petrol station** or a similar area next to the road,
- from a **path, a farm road** (*ägoväg*) or a similar exit road,
- from a **cycle track**, a **pedestrian street**, a **walking-speed area**, a **cycle street**, or from **terrain**,
- **after crossing a footpath or cycle track** – for example when you drive out from a property across the pavement.

You also give way to vehicles on the carriageway when you come onto it from the **shoulder** or from a cycle track that is part of the road [[src:tf|3:21]].

::: tip Recognising an exit
A typical exit has a **raised kerb** or a pavement or cycle track that continues across it, so you have to cross the path to get out. That tells you: give way to everyone.
:::

## Main roads, motorways and expressways

A driver who comes onto a **main road**, **motorway** or **expressway** from another road, where there is **no acceleration lane**, must give way to the vehicles on that road – unless they come on without changing lanes [[src:tf|3:21]].

With an **acceleration lane** (*accelerationsfält*), use it to match the speed of the traffic in the lane you want to enter, and move out of it as soon as you can do so without danger or unnecessary obstruction [[src:tf|3:23]]. Drivers already on the road must adjust their speed to make it easier for you [[src:tf|3:43]]. More in [[lesson:motorways]].

## Roundabouts

::: law tf 3:22
> En förare som kör in i en cirkulationsplats har väjningsplikt mot varje fordon som befinner sig i cirkulationen.

**In English:** "A driver entering a roundabout must give way to every vehicle that is already in the roundabout."
:::

A roundabout is also marked with a give-way or stop sign ([[sign:D3|D3]] with [[sign:B1|B1]] or [[sign:B2|B2]]). Inside it you drive anticlockwise [[src:tf|3:13]]. How to choose lanes and signal is in [[lesson:turning-and-lanes]].

## Turning across traffic

::: law tf 3:24
> En förare som skall svänga i en vägkorsning eller skall lämna eller korsa den körbana han eller hon färdas på skall förvissa sig om att det kan ske utan hinder för mötande trafik eller för trafikanterna på den körbana som föraren avser att köra in på.

**In English:** "A driver who is going to turn at a junction, or leave or cross the carriageway he or she is driving on, must make sure that it can be done without obstructing oncoming traffic or the road users on the carriageway the driver is entering."
:::

So when you **turn left**, you let **oncoming** traffic – going straight or turning right – go first. When you turn and cross a **pedestrian crossing** or a **cycle passage**, drive slowly and let the pedestrians and cyclists cross [[src:tf|3:60]] [[src:tf|3:61]].

## Trains, trams and emergency vehicles

You must **give free way** to **trains**, to **trams** (unless a sign gives the tram driver a duty to give way), and to **emergency vehicles** using siren and blue lights – stopping if necessary [[src:tf|2:5]]. See [[lesson:basic-duties]] and [[lesson:railway-crossings]].

## Buses leaving a bus stop

Where the limit is **50 km/h or lower**, slow down – and stop if needed – to let a **bus** that signals that it is leaving a bus stop pull out. The bus driver must still take special care [[src:tf|3:45]].

## Pedestrians

You must give way to pedestrians [[src:tf|3:58]] [[src:tf|3:61]] [[src:tf|8:1]]:

- who are on or about to step onto an **uncontrolled pedestrian crossing**,
- on **footpaths** and other places meant for pedestrians,
- when you drive out onto the road from a **property exit**,
- on a **pedestrian street** and in a **walking-speed area**.

When you **meet or overtake** pedestrians, give them enough room and time to step aside [[src:tf|3:58]]. When you pass a **tram or bus** stopped at a stop **without** a traffic island, on its right side, you must **stop** and let passengers get on and off [[src:tf|3:58]].

## Cyclists and moped riders

- On a **cycle track**, cyclists and class II moped riders have priority over other drivers [[src:tf|3:59]].
- At a **cycle crossing** (sign [[sign:B8|B8]]) you must give way to cyclists on or about to ride onto it [[src:tf|3:61a]].
- At a **cycle passage** (no B8 sign) you must adapt your speed, and after a turn let them pass [[src:tf|3:61]].
- When you drive onto a **cycle street**, you must give way to the vehicles on it [[src:tf|8:1a]].

See [[lesson:pedestrians-and-cyclists]].

## Do not block the junction

When you approach or enter a junction, drive so that you **will not block** the crossing traffic if you have to stop in the junction [[src:tf|3:20]]. In a queue: wait before the junction until there is room for your whole vehicle on the other side.
