## Drive on the carriageway – and keep right

Vehicles are driven on the **carriageway** (*körbana*). Bicycles and two-wheeled class II mopeds use the **cycle track** if there is one [[src:tf|3:6]]. A path meant for certain traffic – a footpath, a cycle track – may only be **crossed** by other traffic [[src:tf|3:6]].

::: law tf 3:7
> Vid färd på väg skall fordon föras i det körfält som är längst till höger i färdriktningen och som är avsett för fordonet.

**In English:** "When driving on a road, vehicles must be driven in the lane that is furthest to the right in the direction of travel and that is intended for the vehicle."
:::

So the basic rule is: **use the right-hand lane**. Bicycles, mopeds and vehicles built for – or not allowed to go faster than – **40 km/h** must keep as close as possible to the **right edge** of the shoulder or path they use [[src:tf|3:7]].

## When you may choose your lane

There are two exceptions to the keep-right rule [[src:tf|3:7]]:

1. **Lanes signed for destinations.** Where overhead lane signs (*körfältsvägvisare*, [[sign:F8|F8]]) assign lanes to different destinations, you may use the lane that is **most suitable** for where you are going.
2. **Roads with a limit of 70 km/h or lower** that have **at least two marked lanes in your direction**. Here you may use the lane that is most suitable for your onward journey – typical in towns.

::: warning But then you must stay in your lane
On such a road (exception 2), you may change lanes **only** to stop or park, to overtake, or to get into the right lane before turning at a junction [[src:tf|3:9]]. Weaving between lanes to get ahead is not allowed.
:::

## Roads with three or more lanes

On a road with traffic in both directions [[src:tf|3:8]]:

- **Four or more lanes:** you may **not** drive in the lanes to the **left of the centre** – they belong to the oncoming traffic.
- **Three lanes:** you may **not** use the **leftmost** lane.

These rules do not apply on a **one-way** carriageway [[src:tf|3:8]].

## Lane discipline

::: law tf 3:9
> Om flera körfält för färd i samma riktning är skilda åt genom en vägmarkering, skall hela fordonet föras inom ett av de avskilda fälten. Om en heldragen linje skiljer körfälten åt får byte av körfält inte ske.
>
> Om en körbana har två eller flera körfält för färd i samma riktning och det är tät köbildning i alla körfälten, får endast den förare som färdas i körfältet längst till höger byta körfält. Annan förare får dock byta körfält om föraren skall stanna eller parkera, eller om det behövs för att fordonet skall vara placerat i ett lämpligt körfält när det närmar sig en korsning.

**In English:** "If several lanes in the same direction are separated by a road marking, the whole vehicle must be kept within one of the lanes. If a solid line separates the lanes, you may not change lanes. If a carriageway has two or more lanes in the same direction and there are dense queues in all lanes, only the driver in the rightmost lane may change lanes. Other drivers may however change lanes if they are going to stop or park, or if it is needed to get into a suitable lane when approaching a junction."
:::

::: key Changing lanes – the safe way
A lane change, starting from the kerb, or any other sideways move may only be made **without danger or unnecessary obstruction** to others [[src:tf|3:43]]. Check your mirrors and [[term:dodvinkel|blind spot]], signal in good time, and then move smoothly. If someone signals to move into your lane, **adjust your speed** to make it easier for them [[src:tf|3:43]].
:::

A lane intended for **certain vehicles only** – for example a bus lane – may only be **crossed** by other vehicles [[src:tf|3:10]]. Solid lines and restricted areas are explained in [[lesson:road-markings]].

## Meeting other vehicles

When you meet another vehicle, **keep right** and leave a safe **sideways distance** [[src:tf|3:30]]. On a narrow road, the driver who has an **obstacle on their side** must stop if needed to let the other pass [[src:tf|3:30]]. More in [[lesson:meeting-and-overtaking]].

## Islands in the road

You must pass to the **right** of a traffic island or other device in the middle of a two-way carriageway. If the island is to the **right** of the centre, or the carriageway is **one-way**, you may also pass it on the **left**, unless a sign says otherwise [[src:tf|3:13]]. In a roundabout you drive **anticlockwise** around the central island [[src:tf|3:13]].

## Reversing and turning round

You may **reverse or turn round** only if it can be done **without danger or obstruction** to other road users [[src:tf|3:46]]. On a **motorway** and an **expressway** you may never reverse or turn round [[src:tf|9:1]] [[src:tf|9:2]].

::: tip Reversing safely
Reverse slowly and as short a distance as possible. Look back over your shoulder as well as in the mirrors – children and other small road users are easy to miss. If you cannot see, get out and look, or ask someone to guide you. It is often safer to reverse **into** a parking space and drive **out** forwards.
:::
