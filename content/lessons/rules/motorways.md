## Two kinds of fast road

[[signs:E1,E2,E3,E4]]

Both are marked with **green** signs: **[[sign:E1|E1 motorway]]** (*motorväg*) and **[[sign:E3|E3 expressway]]** (*motortrafikled*). A road can only be declared one of them if it has **no level junctions** with other roads and you can only get on and off at its ends or at special slip roads [[src:tf|10:7]].

| | Motorway | Expressway |
|---|---|---|
| Junctions | No level junctions; slip roads only | No level junctions; slip roads only |
| Directions | **Two separate carriageways**, one for each direction, separated by a central reserve or similar [[src:tf|10:7]] | Can have traffic in **both directions on the same carriageway** – often without a barrier |
| Default speed limit | **[[fact:speed.motorway]]** (Trafikverket may set 120 km/h) [[src:tf|9:1]] | **No own limit** – the base speed of 70 km/h applies unless signs show more [[src:tf|3:17]] |

## The special rules

::: law tf 9:1
> På en motorväg gäller följande.
>
> 1. Endast trafik med motorfordon eller motorfordon med tillkopplat fordon som är konstruerade för och får framföras med en hastighet av minst 40 kilometer i timmen får förekomma. Mopeder klass I får dock inte framföras på en motorväg.
> 2. Fordon får föras in på en motorväg endast vid vägens början eller på en påfartsväg och föras av motorvägen endast på en avfartsväg eller vid dess slut.
> 3. Fordon får inte föras på skiljeremsan eller på en tvärgående förbindelseväg mellan körbanorna.
> 4. Fordon får inte vändas eller backas.
> 5. Fordon får inte stannas eller parkeras annat än på platser som utmärkts med ett vägmärke som parkerings- eller rastplats. Bussar i linjetrafik får dock stannas vid särskilt anordnade hållplatser.
> 6. Fordon får inte föras med högre hastighet än 110 kilometer i timmen.

**In English:** "On a motorway: 1. Only motor vehicles, with or without a connected vehicle, that are built for and allowed to go at least 40 km/h may be used. Class I mopeds may however not be driven on a motorway. 2. Vehicles may only enter at the start of the motorway or on a slip road, and leave only on an exit road or at its end. 3. Vehicles may not be driven on the central reserve or on a crossing link between the carriageways. 4. Vehicles may not be turned round or reversed. 5. Vehicles may not be stopped or parked except at places signed as parking or rest areas; scheduled buses may however stop at special stops. 6. Vehicles may not be driven faster than 110 km/h."
:::

Rules **1–5 apply on expressways too**, and on the slip roads to and from them [[src:tf|9:2]]. Rule 6 (110 km/h) applies only to motorways.

::: key So you will never meet …
… pedestrians, cyclists, class I or II mopeds, tractors or other slow vehicles on a motorway or expressway – they are not allowed there [[src:tf|9:1]]. Still be ready for the unexpected: broken-down vehicles, debris, animals, road works.
:::

## Joining

::: steps
1. Follow the slip road and **read the signs** – the speed limit on the slip road may be lower.
2. On the **acceleration lane**, **look over your shoulder and in the mirror** and match your speed to the traffic in the lane you are joining [[src:tf|3:23]].
3. **Signal** and move out as soon as you can do it **without danger or unnecessary obstruction** [[src:tf|3:23]].
4. If there is **no acceleration lane**, you must **give way** to the traffic on the motorway or expressway [[src:tf|3:21]].
:::

Drivers already on the road must adjust their speed to make it easier for someone joining [[src:tf|3:43]] – and if it is safe, moving to the left lane helps.

## Driving

- Keep to the **right-hand lane** unless you are overtaking [[src:tf|3:7]]. After overtaking, move back right when it is safe [[src:tf|3:37]].
- Keep a **long distance** – at 110 km/h you travel about **30 metres every second**. See [[lesson:speed-and-stopping-distance]].
- In dense queues in all lanes, only drivers in the right-hand lane may change lanes (with some exceptions) [[src:tf|3:9]].
- Watch for **lane signals** over the lanes – a red cross means you must leave that lane [[src:vmf|3:15]].
- On an **expressway with oncoming traffic** in the same carriageway, overtaking can be as dangerous as on any two-way road.

## Leaving

Watch for the **advance exit signs** ([[sign:F4|F4]]) – outside built-up areas they stand 300–500 metres before the exit, and on fast roads also at twice that distance [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:12]]. Move into the **right-hand lane in good time**, signal, and move into the **deceleration lane** as soon as possible. Slow down **in the deceleration lane**, not on the motorway [[src:tf|3:23]].

::: warning Missed your exit?
**Never** stop, reverse or turn round. Drive on to the next exit [[src:tf|9:1]].
:::

## If you break down

::: steps
1. Turn on the **hazard warning lights** and, if you can, roll onto the **shoulder** or an **emergency lay-by** ([[sign:E27|E27]]).
2. Put on a **reflective vest** if you have one, and get everyone out on the side **away from the traffic**. Stand **behind the crash barrier** if there is one.
3. Put out the **warning triangle** – required where the limit is above 50 km/h and the vehicle stands on the carriageway or shoulder where stopping is not allowed [[src:tf|3:57]].
4. Call for help. Get the vehicle moved **as soon as possible** [[src:tf|3:51]].
:::

::: only motorcycle
**For riders:** a motorcycle is allowed on motorways – it is a motor vehicle built for well over 40 km/h. Side wind, turbulence from trucks and grooves in the road surface are felt more strongly on a motorcycle at motorway speed. Keep a stable lane position and a long distance to the vehicle in front.
:::
