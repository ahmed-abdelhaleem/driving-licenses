## Meeting other vehicles

When you meet another vehicle, **keep to the right** and leave a **safe sideways distance** [[src:tf|3:30]].

::: law tf 3:30
> Om det finns något hinder för möte skall den förare stanna som har hindret på sin sida, om det är nödvändigt för att den mötande skall kunna passera.

**In English:** "If there is an obstacle to meeting, the driver who has the obstacle on his or her side must stop, if necessary, so that the oncoming vehicle can pass."
:::

So if a parked car blocks **your** side of a narrow street, **you** wait. On narrow roads without an obstacle, both drivers keep as far right as is safe and slow down [[src:tf|3:15]]. Signs [[sign:B6|B6]] and [[sign:B7|B7]] can decide who goes first – see [[lesson:priority-signs]].

## Overtaking – the basic rules

*Overtaking* (*omkörning*) means passing a vehicle that travels in the same direction. Normally you overtake on the **left** [[src:tf|3:31]].

Before you start, **all** of these must be true:

::: steps
1. **It can be done without danger** [[src:tf|3:32]].
2. **The lane you will use is free** of oncoming traffic and other obstacles for a **long enough distance** [[src:tf|3:36]].
3. **You can get back** to the right part of the road afterwards without danger to others [[src:tf|3:36]].
4. **Nobody behind you** has started to overtake you [[src:tf|3:32]].
5. **The driver in front** has not signalled to overtake or to move left [[src:tf|3:32]].
6. **No ban** applies – no sign [[sign:C27|C27]], no solid line on your side, and none of the places listed below.
:::

While overtaking, leave a **safe sideways distance** to the vehicle you pass [[src:tf|3:33]]. Afterwards, move back to the right as soon as it is safe and convenient [[src:tf|3:37]]. On a road with two or more lanes in your direction you do not have to move back if you will soon overtake again or turn left, and it does not inconvenience traffic behind [[src:tf|3:37]].

::: key Overtaking a cyclist
Give a cyclist plenty of room – they may wobble or swerve around a pothole. If you cannot leave a safe distance because of oncoming traffic, **wait behind**.
:::

## Overtaking on the right

You **must** overtake on the **right** when the vehicle in front **turns left** or clearly prepares to do so [[src:tf|3:31]]. Cyclists and moped riders may overtake vehicles other than bicycles and mopeds on the right [[src:tf|3:31]].

**Trains and trams** on the road are overtaken on the **right**, unless the position of the rails makes the left necessary; on a one-way carriageway you may also overtake them on the left if the traffic allows [[src:tf|3:35]].

## Where you may not overtake

::: law tf 3:39
> Omkörning får inte ske strax före eller i en vägkorsning, utom när
>
> 1. omkörningen skall ske till höger,
> 2. trafiken i vägkorsningen är reglerad av en polisman eller genom trafiksignaler, eller
> 3. det framgår av vägmärken eller något annat förhållande att förarna på den korsande vägen har väjningsplikt eller stopplikt.

**In English:** "Overtaking is not allowed just before or in a junction, except when 1. the overtaking is on the right, 2. the junction is controlled by a police officer or traffic lights, or 3. signs or something else show that drivers on the crossing road must give way or stop."
:::

Overtaking is also forbidden [[src:tf|3:39]]:

- **just before or on a level crossing**, unless it has barriers or is controlled by the same kind of traffic lights as a junction,
- at a **hill crest or bend with limited view**, unless you have at least two lanes in your direction where oncoming traffic may not come, or can overtake without using a lane meant for oncoming traffic.

The same section ends with an exception: **two-wheeled vehicles without a sidecar** – bicycles, two-wheeled mopeds and motorcycles – may still be overtaken [[src:tf|3:39]], but only when it is safe [[src:tf|3:32]].

There is **no** such exception here: you may not overtake any vehicle **just before or on an uncontrolled pedestrian crossing**, an uncontrolled **cycle passage** or a **cycle crossing** [[src:tf|3:40]].

::: warning A queue at a pedestrian crossing
If the car in front has stopped before a pedestrian crossing, it may be stopping for a pedestrian you cannot see. Never pass it. Even passing in another lane where this does not count as overtaking (see below), you must go so slowly that you can stop at once if someone is on the crossing [[src:tf|3:41]].
:::

## When someone overtakes you

If you notice that someone is going to overtake you on the left, keep **as far right as possible** and do **not speed up** or make it harder in any other way [[src:tf|3:38]]. If you drive a slow or wide vehicle on a narrow, winding road or one with oncoming traffic, slow down and pull aside as soon as possible to let others pass [[src:tf|3:38]]. And keep a gap to the vehicle in front so others can overtake you in steps [[src:tf|3:2]].

## When passing is not overtaking

In these cases, passing vehicles in another lane does **not** count as overtaking [[src:tf|3:41]]:

1. there are two or more lanes in your direction and **dense queues** in all of them,
2. the lanes are signed for **different destinations** with overhead lane signs, or
3. vehicles have taken **different lanes before a junction**.

Then you may pass on the right as well. But before a pedestrian crossing, cycle passage or cycle crossing, you must pass so slowly that you can stop immediately [[src:tf|3:41]].

## No-overtaking signs

Sign [[sign:C27|C27]] bans overtaking motor vehicles other than two-wheeled mopeds and motorcycles without a sidecar, until sign [[sign:C28|C28]] [[src:vmf|2:8]]. Solid lines are covered in [[lesson:road-markings]].

::: only motorcycle
**For riders:** a motorcycle accelerates quickly, which makes overtaking tempting. The same rules apply to you as to cars. Plan the overtake, check the space you will return to, and remember that a motorcycle is small – oncoming drivers can easily misjudge its distance and speed.
:::
