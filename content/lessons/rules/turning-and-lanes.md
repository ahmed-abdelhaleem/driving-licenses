## Choose your lane in good time

::: law tf 3:25
> Där körbanan före en vägkorsning har två eller flera körfält i samma färdriktning skall föraren i god tid placera sitt fordon i körfältet längst till höger om föraren skall svänga till höger, och i körfältet längst till vänster om han eller hon skall svänga till vänster. Vid färd rakt fram skall föraren placera fordonet i det körfält som är lämpligast med hänsyn till övrig trafik och den fortsatta färden.

**In English:** "Where the carriageway before a junction has two or more lanes in the same direction, the driver must in good time place the vehicle in the rightmost lane to turn right, and in the leftmost lane to turn left. To go straight on, the driver must use the lane that is most suitable with regard to other traffic and the onward journey."
:::

| You will … | Use … |
|---|---|
| turn **right** | the **rightmost** lane |
| turn **left** | the **leftmost** lane |
| go **straight on** | the lane that suits the traffic and your onward journey |

A long vehicle may deviate from this, with special care, if there is not enough room in the junction otherwise [[src:tf|3:25]]. Signs ([[sign:F21|F21]], [[sign:F8|F8]]) and lane arrows ([[sign:M19|M19]]) often tell you which lane to use. Where the lanes are separated by **solid lines**, you **must** follow the arrows [[src:vmf|4:9]].

## Look back before you turn

::: law tf 3:26
> En förare får svänga i en vägkorsning endast om det kan ske utan fara eller onödigt hinder för andra som färdas i samma riktning. Särskild uppmärksamhet skall riktas på den bakomvarande trafiken.

**In English:** "A driver may turn at a junction only if it can be done without danger or unnecessary obstruction for others travelling in the same direction. Special attention must be paid to traffic behind."
:::

::: steps
1. **Mirror and blind spot** – especially on the side you turn to. Cyclists and moped riders can be beside you.
2. **Signal** in good time [[src:tf|3:65]].
3. **Position** – move to the correct place on the road (see below).
4. **Speed** – slow down so you can give way and see.
5. **Look again** just before you turn – then turn.
:::

### Turning right

Keep as close to the **right edge** of the carriageway as is suitable [[src:tf|3:26]]. Then a cyclist cannot end up on your right.

::: warning Cyclists on your right
Cyclists and class II moped riders may keep to the right before a junction even when they are going **left** – they ride across to the far side and turn there [[src:tf|3:28]]. When you turn right, a cyclist going straight on or waiting on your right is in great danger. Check your mirror and **blind spot** ([[term:dodvinkel]]) before turning, and give way on cycle crossings and passages [[src:tf|3:61]] [[src:tf|3:61a]].
:::

### Turning left

Keep as close to the **centre** of the carriageway as possible – or, on a one-way road, as close to the **left edge** as possible. When you leave the junction, you must be on the **right side** of the road you turn into [[src:tf|3:26]]. Let oncoming traffic pass first [[src:tf|3:24]].

Two vehicles coming from opposite directions that **both turn left** may pass each other on the **left** – in front of each other – if it can be done without danger or inconvenience [[src:tf|3:27]].

### Turning into a road with several lanes

When you turn into a road that has two or more lanes in your direction, turn in the way that best suits the other traffic and your onward journey [[src:tf|3:27]]. All of this also applies when you cross or leave the road somewhere other than a junction, for example into a driveway [[src:tf|3:29]].

## Roundabouts

A **roundabout** (*cirkulationsplats*) is a place that the authorities have decided is a roundabout, marked with sign [[sign:D3|D3]] [[src:ts-roundabout]]. (In Swedish, *rondell* means only the island in the middle.) The law has only a few rules for roundabouts: you give way to vehicles already in it, and you drive **anticlockwise** [[src:tf|3:22]] [[src:tf|3:13]]. Transportstyrelsen's official guide explains the rest [[src:ts-roundabout]]:

### On the way in

- **Give way** to vehicles that are already in the roundabout.
- **Choose the lane** that is most suitable for your onward journey. If there are signs or markings, follow them.
- There is **no requirement to signal** when you drive in – there is only one direction to drive.
- Long vehicles often need **more than one lane**. Give them room.

### In the roundabout

- You may only **change lanes** if it can be done without danger or undue obstruction.
- When you change lanes or move sideways, you **must signal**.
- If you see a driver about to move into your lane, **adjust your speed** to make it easier.

### On the way out

- When you leave the roundabout, you **must signal right**.
- Try to plan so that you leave from the **right-hand lane**.
- If you leave from the **left-hand lane**, take extra care with vehicles in the right-hand lane – they may be going round further. Remember that you are **changing lanes**.

::: example Choosing a lane in a two-lane roundabout
- **First exit** (to the right): right-hand lane, signal right as you approach your exit.
- **Straight on:** the lane that suits you best – often the right-hand lane.
- **Exits further round:** often the left-hand lane; move to the right-hand lane when it is safe, before your exit, and signal right to leave.

This is one example. The official guide says you may choose differently if it suits the situation better, and signs and markings always come first [[src:ts-roundabout]].
:::

## Joining and leaving fast roads

On an **acceleration lane**, adapt your speed to the traffic in the lane you are entering, and move out of the acceleration lane as soon as you can do so without danger or unnecessary obstruction. When you want to leave a road, move in good time into the lane best suited for it, and into the **deceleration lane** as soon as possible if there is one [[src:tf|3:23]]. See [[lesson:motorways]].

When two lanes merge into one, drivers must adapt to each other with **mutual consideration** [[src:tf|3:44]] – see [[sign:E15|E15]] in [[lesson:information-signs]].
