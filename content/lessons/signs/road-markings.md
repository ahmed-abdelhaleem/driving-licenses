## Markings are white – with some exceptions

Road markings (*vägmarkeringar*) regulate, warn or guide, alone or together with signs [[src:vmf|4:2]]. They are **white** unless something else is stated [[src:vmf|4:3]]. Two kinds of yellow marking exist:

- **Temporary yellow lines** at road works. They apply **instead of** the ordinary white markings [[src:vmf|4:3]]. If you see both, follow the yellow.
- **Yellow stopping and parking lines** at the kerb ([[sign:M21|M21]]–[[sign:M23|M23]]), explained below.

## Broken lines along the road

[[signs:M1,M3,M2,M4]]

The **ratio between line and gap** tells you which line it is [[src:vmf|4:4]]:

| Marking | Line : gap | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **[[sign:M1|M1 centre line or lane line]]** | 1:3 or 1:1 – **short lines, long gaps** | A **centre line** separates lanes for opposite directions; a **lane line** separates lanes in the same direction. |
| **[[sign:M3|M3 warning line]]** | 3:1 – **long lines, short gaps** | Separates opposite directions where it is **unsuitable to cross** because of the traffic conditions. It can also warn that a **solid line is coming**. |
| **[[sign:M2|M2 edge line]]** | 1:2 | Marks the outer edge of the carriageway. |
| **[[sign:M4|M4 guide line]]** | 1:1 | Shows a suitable path through a complicated junction. |

::: key Warning line = think twice
Crossing a **warning line** is not forbidden, but it tells you the road ahead is not suitable for it – for example for overtaking. The more paint you see, the more careful you must be: short dashes → long dashes → solid line.
:::

[[signs:M5,M6,M7]]

- **[[sign:M5|M5 cycle lane line]]** – between a cycle lane and another lane [[src:vmf|4:4]].
- **[[sign:M6|M6 bus lane line]]** – between a lane for buses (vehicles in scheduled services) and another lane [[src:vmf|4:4]].
- **[[sign:M7|M7 reversible lane]]** – borders a lane that is opened for traffic in one direction or the other at different times [[src:vmf|4:4]].

## Solid lines and restricted areas

[[signs:M8,M9]]

Solid lines are used instead of broken lines where, for safety reasons, it is **not allowed** to cross into the opposite lane, change lanes or drive out onto the shoulder [[src:vmf|4:5]]. A solid centre line can be single or double [[src:vmf|4:6]]. **[[sign:M9|M9 restricted area]]** (*spärrområde*) is an area marked with angled or diagonal stripes inside a solid line [[src:vtdf|2]].

::: law tf 3:11
> Ett fordon får inte med något hjul föras på ett spärrområde eller över en heldragen linje.
>
> Om särskild försiktighet iakttas får dock undantag göras från detta om
>
> 1. det behövs för att fordonet skall kunna passera ett hinder på vägen,
> 2. utrymmet i en vägkorsning annars inte är tillräckligt för fordonet,
> 3. en heldragen linje behöver korsas för färd till eller från en fastighet eller motsvarande, eller
> 4. det i fordonets färdriktning löper en streckad linje omedelbart till höger om den heldragna linjen.

**In English:** "A vehicle may not be driven with any wheel on a restricted area or over a solid line. With special care, exceptions may however be made if 1. it is needed to get past an obstacle on the road, 2. there is otherwise not enough room in a junction for the vehicle, 3. a solid line has to be crossed to drive to or from a property or similar, or 4. a broken line runs immediately to the right of the solid line, seen in the vehicle's direction of travel."
:::

So **not one wheel** on or over a solid line or a restricted area – unless one of the four exceptions applies, and then only with special care. A solid **edge line** may be crossed when you are one of the road users who must use the shoulder, such as a cyclist [[src:tf|3:11]] [[src:tf|3:12]].

### Combined lines

[[signs:M10,M11,M12]]

- **[[sign:M10|M10]]** – a centre or lane line together with a solid line.
- **[[sign:M11|M11]]** – a warning line together with a solid line.
- **[[sign:M12|M12]]** – a centre line together with a warning line.

With M10 and M11, **the line nearest to you decides**: if the **solid** line is on your side, you may **not** cross; if the **broken** line is on your side, you may cross it (and the solid line beyond it) – with care [[src:vmf|4:7]] [[src:tf|3:11]]. With M12, crossing is allowed but unsuitable from the side with the warning line [[src:vmf|4:7]].

### Solid lines and stopping

You may not stop or park along a **solid line between lanes** if there would be less than **3 metres** between your vehicle and the line – unless a broken line runs between you and the solid line [[src:tf|3:53]]. Otherwise the others would have to cross the solid line to pass you.

## The hard shoulder

The shoulder (*vägren*) is the part of the road outside the edge line. It **must** be used – if it is wide enough and suitable – by cyclists and class II moped riders when there is no cycle track, by class I mopeds, by vehicles that cannot or may not go faster than 40 km/h, and by horse-drawn vehicles, riders and people driving animals [[src:tf|3:12]].

::: law tf 3:12
> Om det behövs för att underlätta framkomligheten för annan trafik, får vägrenen användas tillfälligt även vid färd med andra fordon.
>
> Har körbanan avgränsats med en heldragen kantlinje får vägrenen användas endast i de fall som anges i första stycket.

**In English:** "If it is needed to make it easier for other traffic to get past, the shoulder may also be used temporarily by other vehicles. If the carriageway is bordered by a solid edge line, the shoulder may only be used in the cases listed in the first paragraph [the slow road users listed above]."
:::

So you may move onto the shoulder for a moment to let a faster vehicle pass you – but **not** if the edge line is **solid**. When you come back from the shoulder onto the carriageway, you must give way to the vehicles there [[src:tf|3:21]].

## Lines across the road

[[signs:M13,M14,M15,M16,M17-1,M17a]]

- **[[sign:M13|M13 stop line]]** – where you must stop for a stop sign, a traffic signal or a level-crossing signal [[src:vmf|4:8]].
- **[[sign:M14|M14 give-way line]]** – white triangles ("shark's teeth") showing the line you should not pass while giving way [[src:vmf|4:8]].
- **[[sign:M15|M15 pedestrian crossing]]** – the zebra stripes, used where the B3 sign stands [[src:vmf|4:8]].
- **[[sign:M16|M16 cycle passage or cycle crossing]]** – square blocks. At a cycle **crossing** (with the B8 sign) it is combined with the give-way line M14 [[src:vmf|4:8]]. See [[lesson:priority-signs]].
- **[[sign:M17|M17 speed bump]]** – marks a hump, dip or similar [[src:vmf|4:8]].
- **[[sign:M17a|M17a cycle box]]** – a marked box at a junction showing a suitable place for cyclists and class II moped riders to wait [[src:vmf|4:8]]. As a driver, stop at your stop line and leave the box free.

## Arrows and symbols on the road

[[signs:M18,M19-1,M19a,M20-1,M20a]]

- **[[sign:M18|M18]]** – a large triangle: a give-way or stop rule is coming further ahead [[src:vmf|4:9]].
- **[[sign:M19|M19 lane arrows]]** – show the suitable lane for the next junction [[src:vmf|4:9]] (M19a before a roundabout).
- **[[sign:M20|M20 change lanes]]** – you must change lanes **as soon as possible** [[src:vmf|4:9]]. **M20a** warns that a solid centre line or a physical barrier is coming [[src:vmf|4:9]].

::: law vmf 4:9 M19
> Är körfältet avgränsat med heldragen körfältslinje ska förare följa den eller de riktningar som pilen visar. … Markeringarna upplyser också om att fordon inte får föras i motsatt riktning.

**In English:** "If the lane is bordered by a solid lane line, drivers must follow the direction or directions the arrow shows. … The markings also show that vehicles may not be driven in the opposite direction."
:::

So lane arrows are **advice** between broken lane lines, but an **order** when the lanes are separated by **solid** lines.

[[signs:M25,M26,M27,M28,M29,M30,M31,M32,M33,M34]]

Text and symbols on the road repeat or support other information: paths for pedestrians and cyclists (M25–M27), **BUSS** for a bus lane (M28), the **speed limit** (M29), a road number (M30), a special-purpose place such as *LASTPLATS* (M31), **STOPP** together with a stop sign (M32), a **disabled parking** space (M33), and other important information such as *SKOLA* (school) (M34) [[src:vmf|4:9]].

## Yellow lines at the kerb

[[signs:M21,M22,M23]]

- **[[sign:M21|M21 solid yellow line]]** – **no stopping or parking** [[src:vmf|4:9]].
- **[[sign:M22|M22 broken yellow line]]** – **no parking**. It can also show the extent of a bus stop [[src:vmf|4:9]].
- **[[sign:M23|M23 yellow zigzag line]]** – reinforces M21 or M22 [[src:vmf|4:9]].

These lines are always yellow and are placed at the edge of the carriageway [[src:vmf|4:9]]. [[sign:M24|M24]] marks the spaces where vehicles may be parked; when such markings exist, no wheel may stand outside them [[src:tf|3:49]].
