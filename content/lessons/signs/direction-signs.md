## The colour tells you the kind of road

Direction signs (*lokaliseringsmärken för vägvisning*) show the way to places. Their **background colour** tells you what kind of road or destination the sign leads to [[src:vmf|2:14]]:

| Background | Leads along or to … |
|---|---|
| **Green**, white text | a **motorway** or **expressway** |
| **Blue**, white text | a **public road** |
| **Yellow with a red border**, black text | a **private road** (*enskild väg*) |
| **White**, black text | a **local destination** in a town, or an institution or facility |
| **Brown**, white text | a destination of **tourist interest** |
| **Orange**, black text | a **temporary diversion** |
| **Yellow**, green text | a temporary event |

A destination can have its own coloured **plate** inside a sign – for example a green plate for a motorway inside a blue sign [[src:tsfs-2019-74|7:2]]. In towns, signs are normally white, but on through public roads they are blue or green [[src:tsfs-2019-74|7:1]].

## Before and at a junction

[[signs:F1,F3,F5,F6,F9]]

::: steps
1. **[[sign:F1|F1 advance direction sign]]** – a map-like sign that tells you about the junction or roundabout ahead [[src:vmf|2:17]]. It stands **50–100 m** before the junction in a built-up area and **300–400 m** before it outside built-up areas [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:5]]. Use it to choose your direction and lane in good time.
2. **[[sign:F3|F3 advance direction sign in table form]]** – the same information as a list, with all directions in the junction [[src:vmf|2:17]].
3. **[[sign:F5|F5 direction sign]]** – an arrow-shaped sign **at the junction** pointing towards the destination [[src:vmf|2:17]]. Distances on it are in whole kilometres.
4. **[[sign:F6|F6 direction sign in table form]]** – can replace F5, placed just before the junction or roundabout, or at the exit from a roundabout [[src:vmf|2:17]].
:::

- **[[sign:F9|F9 collective direction sign]]** – the destinations in the bracket are all reached by the same choice, shown under the point of the bracket [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F2|F2]]** – an advance sign that tells you that a turn is **banned** in the junction ahead, and shows the other way to get there [[src:vmf|2:17]].

## Lanes

[[signs:F8,F21-1,F16-1,F17-1,F18-1,F19-1,F20-1]]

- **[[sign:F8|F8 lane direction sign]]** – hangs **over** the lanes and shows which lane is suitable for each destination [[src:vmf|2:17]]. Where lanes are assigned to destinations like this, you may use the lane that suits your route best [[src:tf|3:7]], and passing cars in the other lanes is **not** overtaking [[src:tf|3:41]].
- **[[sign:F21|F21 lanes before a junction]]** – shows which lane to use for each direction at the next junction [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F16|F16 more lanes ahead]]** and **[[sign:F17|F17 fewer lanes ahead]]** – the number of lanes increases or decreases [[src:vmf|2:17]]. F17 is always used where the limit is 70 km/h or more, also as advance warning [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:27]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:28]].
- **[[sign:F18|F18 lane layout]]**, **[[sign:F19|F19 joining road with acceleration lane]]** and **[[sign:F20|F20 joining road with its own lane]]** show how lanes run and how another road joins [[src:vmf|2:17]].

::: tip Choose your lane early
Read F1 and F8 in good time, move into the right lane before the junction, and then stay in it. If you end up in the wrong lane, follow it and find another way back – a sudden lane change at the last moment can surprise the drivers around you. The general lane rules are in [[lesson:turning-and-lanes]].
:::

## Motorway exits

[[signs:F4,F7,F27]]

- **[[sign:F4|F4 advance exit sign]]** – warns that an exit is coming [[src:vmf|2:17]]. Outside built-up areas it stands **300–500 m** before the exit; where the limit is 70 km/h or more, it is also put up at **double that distance** [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:12]].
- **[[sign:F7|F7 exit sign]]** – stands where the exit **begins** [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F27|F27 junction number]]** – the number of the interchange (*trafikplats*) [[src:vmf|2:17]]. Useful when you follow a route description or a navigator.

When you want to leave the road, move into the lane best suited for it in good time, and if there is a deceleration lane, move into it as soon as possible [[src:tf|3:23]].

## Places, numbers and distances

[[signs:F10,F13,F14-1,F14-3,F14-2,F11,F22]]

- **[[sign:F10|F10 place name sign]]** – the name of a town or another place useful for orientation. At a municipal or county border, the name is followed by *kommun* or *län* [[src:vmf|2:17]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:23]].

::: warning A place name is not a built-up area sign
F10 only tells you the name of the place. It does **not** start the 50 km/h built-up area rule – only [[sign:E5|E5]] does that.
:::

- **[[sign:F13|F13 distance sign]]** – distances in whole kilometres. The furthest destination is at the top and the nearest at the bottom [[src:vmf|2:17]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|8:24]].
- **[[sign:F14|F14 road number]]** – only roads numbered 1–499 are shown. **Green** plates with an **E** are European roads (for example E4); **blue** plates are national or county roads. A **dashed border** means the road **leads to** the road with that number [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F11|F11 road name]]**, [[sign:F12|F12 watercourse]] and **[[sign:F22|F22 national border]]** – the border to Sweden or another EU country [[src:vmf|2:17]].

## Diversions and road works

[[signs:F15,F23,F24,F25-1,F26-1,F33]]

- **[[sign:F15|F15 permanent diversion route]]** – a fixed alternative route for a numbered road [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F23|F23]]** and **[[sign:F24|F24]]** – a **temporary** diversion when the normal road is closed: F23 gives advance warning and F24 shows the direction [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F25|F25 lane closed]]** – a lane is temporarily closed, and **another lane in your direction can be used** – move over [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F26|F26 lane closed]]** – a lane is temporarily closed and **no other lane in your direction can be used**, so traffic is led into the opposite lane [[src:vmf|2:17]].
- **[[sign:F33|F33 rescue point]]** – a numbered rescue point at road works or similar [[src:vmf|2:17]]. The number makes the place easy to identify if you need to call for help.

## Other F signs

[[signs:F28,F29-1,F30,F31,F32,F38]]

[[sign:F28|F28 multi-storey car park]], [[sign:F29|F29 park and ride]] (parking next to public transport), [[sign:F30|F30 local loop]], [[sign:F31|F31 suitable route for a vehicle type]], [[sign:F32|F32 dangerous goods route]] and [[sign:F38|F38 cycle route]]. Signs F34–F37 are smaller direction signs for pedestrians and cyclists, used on footpaths and cycle tracks [[src:vmf|2:18]].

## Facility, service and tourist signs

[[signs:G1,G2,G4,G9,H1,H3,H5,H11,H13,H27]]

- **G signs** show public facilities (*allmänna inrättningar*), for example a post office, an emergency telephone, a hospital with an emergency department, a railway station or a ferry [[src:vmf|2:22]].
- **H signs** show services (*serviceanläggningar*), for example information, fuel, a café, a campsite, a rest area or a **charging station** for electric vehicles [[src:vmf|2:24]].
- Both are blue with a white square, and they can be built into direction signs [[src:vmf|2:21]] [[src:vmf|2:23]].

[[signs:I1,I2,I3,I4]]

**I signs** (brown) show tourist routes, tourist areas, landmarks and UNESCO **World Heritage Sites** [[src:vmf|2:26]].

## Notice signs and symbols

[[signs:J2,J3]]

- **[[sign:J2|J2 information sign]]** – important information for traffic flow or safety that cannot be given in any other way, for example that road salting ends (*Vägsaltning upphör*) [[src:vmf|2:27]].
- **[[sign:J3|J3 dangerous overhead power line]]** – an electric line over the road or route that can be dangerous [[src:vmf|2:27]]. Think of it with a high load, a crane or a boat on a trailer.

[[signs:S1,S3,S4,S5,S7,S8,S10,S11]]

**Symbols** (S) show vehicle types or road-user groups on direction signs and panels. For example **[[sign:S7|S7]]** means two-wheeled motorcycles and two-wheeled class I mopeds without a sidecar, and **[[sign:S8|S8]]** bicycles and class II mopeds [[src:vmf|2:28]]. Under a sign, a panel with a symbol means the sign **only** applies to that vehicle type – see [[lesson:additional-panels]].
