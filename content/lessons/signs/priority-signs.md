## What "give way" means

Priority signs (*väjningspliktsmärken*) tell you who must let whom go first. The key word is [[term:vajningsplikt|väjningsplikt – the duty to give way]].

::: law tf 3:5
> Förare som har väjningsplikt skall tydligt visa sin avsikt att väja genom att i god tid sänka hastigheten eller stanna.
>
> Föraren får köra vidare endast om det med beaktande av andra trafikanters placering, avståndet till dem och deras hastighet inte uppkommer fara eller hinder.

**In English:** "A driver who has to give way must clearly show the intention to give way by reducing speed or stopping in good time. The driver may continue only if, taking into account the position of other road users, the distance to them and their speed, this causes no danger or obstruction."
:::

Two things are required:

1. **Show it early.** Slow down clearly – or stop – in good time, so the others can see that you have understood and will let them pass.
2. **Only go when it is free.** You may drive on only when you cause no danger **and no obstruction** – for example, you must not make anyone brake or swerve because of you. Hindering others counts, not just crashing.

## B1 Give way

[[signs:B1]]

[[sign:B1|B1]] tells you to give way to vehicles on the crossing road [[src:vmf|2:6]]. It stands as close as possible before the edge of the crossing road, and never more than 25 metres from it [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:4]]. On the road there is often a **give-way line** of white triangles, [[sign:M14|M14]], which shows the line you should not pass while you give way [[src:vmf|4:8]].

The same sign is used with panels to give advance warning:

[[signs:T2,T3,T13,T15-1]]

- **B1 + [[sign:T2|T2 distance]]**: you will have to give way after that distance.
- **B1 + [[sign:T3|T3 distance to stop]]**: a **stop sign** comes after that distance [[src:vmf|2:30]]. It is used where the crossing road is hard to discover in time, or where main roads cross at more than 50 km/h [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:9]].
- **B1 + [[sign:T13|T13 all-way give way]]**: **everyone** entering the junction has to give way [[src:vmf|2:30]]. Then nobody has priority from the signs – be extra careful and agree with the others by eye contact and clear driving.
- **B1 + [[sign:T15|T15]]**: shows how the roads continue. Drivers on the **narrow** line give way to vehicles on the **wide** line [[src:vmf|2:30]]. It is used, for example, where you also have to give way to traffic turning from the opposite direction [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:5]].

## B2 Stop

[[signs:B2]]

[[sign:B2|B2 stop]] (*stopplikt*) means you must **stop completely** before you enter the crossing road, track or railway area, and then give way [[src:vmf|2:6]]. You must stop even if you can see that the road is empty.

::: law tf 3:19
> En förare som har stopplikt eller skall stanna på grund av trafiksignal skall stanna vid stopplinje. Om stopplinje saknas skall föraren stanna omedelbart före signalen eller innan föraren kör in på den korsande vägen, leden eller spårområdet.
>
> Bestämmelserna i 5 § andra stycket gäller även förare som har stopplikt.

**In English:** "A driver who has a duty to stop, or who must stop because of a traffic signal, must stop at the stop line. If there is no stop line, the driver must stop immediately before the signal or before entering the crossing road, route or track area. The rule in section 5, second paragraph [drive on only without danger or obstruction] also applies to drivers who have a duty to stop."
:::

::: steps
1. Stop **at the stop line** ([[sign:M13|M13]]) – the word **STOPP** ([[sign:M32|M32]]) may be painted on the road as well [[src:vmf|4:9]].
2. If you **cannot see** from the stop line, roll forward slowly and look again. You have already done your stop.
3. If there is **no stop line**, stop just before you enter the crossing road [[src:tf|3:19]].
4. Drive on only when it is free, exactly as when you give way.
:::

With [[sign:T14|T14 all-way stop]], drivers on **all** approaches have to stop [[src:vmf|2:30]].

## B4 Main road and B5 End of main road

[[signs:B4,B5]]

A **main road** ([[term:huvudled|huvudled]]) is a road with priority. [[sign:B4|B4]] stands where the main road begins and is repeated after each junction, unless it is clear anyway that the main road continues [[src:vmf|2:6]].

::: law tf 3:21
> En förare som från en väg kör in på en annan väg som är huvudled, motorväg eller motortrafikled och där accelerationsfält saknas, har väjningsplikt mot fordon på den väg föraren kör in på. Väjningsplikten gäller dock inte där föraren kommer in på huvudleden, motorvägen eller motortrafikleden utan att byta körfält.

**In English:** "A driver who drives from one road onto another road that is a main road, motorway or expressway, where there is no acceleration lane, must give way to vehicles on the road the driver is entering. The duty to give way does not apply where the driver comes onto the main road, motorway or expressway without changing lanes."
:::

So on a main road, traffic **from the side roads gives way to you**. The side roads normally also have a B1 or B2 sign. Before a complicated junction you may see [[sign:A29|A29]], which shows your road as the wide line [[src:tsfs-2019-74|2:12]].

::: warning Two rules on a main road
- You may **not park** on a main road [[src:tf|3:55]]. Stopping briefly, for example to let someone out, is allowed where nothing else forbids it.
- Having priority does **not** mean you can ignore others. If someone pulls out in front of you, you must still do what you can to avoid a collision [[src:tf|2:1]].
:::

[[sign:B5|B5 end of main road]] shows where the priority ends. It is not needed where the main road ends at a junction where you get a give-way or stop sign yourself, or where a motorway or expressway begins [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:13]]. After B5, the normal rules – often the right-hand rule – apply again.

## B6 and B7 – narrow passages

[[signs:B6,B7]]

- [[sign:B6|B6 give way to oncoming traffic]]: you may not drive into the stretch ahead if doing so would **hinder vehicles coming towards you** [[src:vmf|2:6]]. Wait until they have passed.
- [[sign:B7|B7 oncoming traffic must give way]]: you have priority on the narrow stretch [[src:vmf|2:6]]. It is only used where B6 stands for the other direction [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:16]].

B6 is only used where the whole stretch can be seen from its end, even in darkness [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:14]]. Without these signs, the general rule applies: **the driver who has the obstacle on their side** must stop if necessary to let the oncoming vehicle pass [[src:tf|3:30]].

## B3 Pedestrian crossing

[[signs:B3-1,B3-2]]

[[sign:B3|B3]] marks a [[term:overgangsstalle|pedestrian crossing]] (*övergångsställe*). The two versions mean the same thing [[src:vmf|2:6]]. The road is painted with white stripes, [[sign:M15|M15]] [[src:vmf|4:8]].

::: law tf 3:61
> Vid ett obevakat övergångsställe har en förare väjningsplikt mot gående som gått ut på eller just ska gå ut på övergångsstället.

**In English:** "At an uncontrolled pedestrian crossing a driver must give way to pedestrians who have stepped onto, or are just about to step onto, the crossing."
:::

- An **uncontrolled** crossing (*obevakat*) has no traffic lights and no police officer directing traffic [[src:vtdf|2]]. You must give way to anyone who is on the crossing **or about to step onto it** [[src:tf|3:61]].
- A **controlled** crossing (*bevakat*) has traffic lights or a police officer [[src:vtdf|2]]. Even when you have green, you must let pedestrians who stepped out correctly finish crossing. After turning at a junction, drive slowly and give way to pedestrians on or about to step onto the crossing [[src:tf|3:60]].
- Approach so that you will **not have to stop on the crossing** [[src:tf|3:62]].
- You may **not overtake** just before or on an uncontrolled crossing [[src:tf|3:40]].
- You may not stop or park on the crossing or within **10 metres before** it [[src:tf|3:53]].

On roads with a limit higher than 60 km/h, a B3 crossing may only be set up where the traffic is controlled by traffic lights [[src:tsfs-2019-74|3:11]].

## B8 Cycle crossing

[[signs:B8]]

[[sign:B8|B8]] marks a [[term:cykeloverfart|cycle crossing]] (*cykelöverfart*). It is painted with square markings ([[sign:M16|M16]]) together with a give-way line ([[sign:M14|M14]]) [[src:vmf|4:8]].

::: law tf 3:61a
> Vid en cykelöverfart har en förare väjningsplikt mot cyklande och förare av moped klass II som är ute på eller just ska färdas ut på cykelöverfarten.

**In English:** "At a cycle crossing a driver must give way to cyclists and riders of class II mopeds who are on, or are just about to ride onto, the cycle crossing."
:::

::: key Cycle crossing or cycle passage?
- A **cycle crossing** (*cykelöverfart*) has the **B8 sign**. **You give way** to the cyclists [[src:tf|3:61a]].
- A **cycle passage** (*cykelpassage*) has only the painted markings and **no B8 sign** [[src:vtdf|2]]. Cyclists must slow down, watch for vehicles and cross only when it is safe [[src:tf|6:6]]. You must still **adapt your speed** so there is no danger for cyclists who are already on it – and after turning at a junction or leaving a roundabout, drive slowly and let cyclists who are on or about to ride onto the passage go first [[src:tf|3:61]].
:::

## When there are no signs

If two vehicles' paths cross and there are no signs, signals or other rules, the **right-hand rule** ([[term:hogerregeln]]) applies: give way to vehicles coming from your right [[src:tf|3:18]]. [[sign:A28|A28]] can warn of such a junction. The [[lesson:right-of-way]] lesson goes through all the right-of-way rules.

Remember the order from [[lesson:signs-basics]]: a **traffic light** that is working with a steady light overrides the give-way and stop signs, and a **police officer** overrides everything [[src:tf|2:2]] [[src:tf|2:3]].
