## Who goes first: police, signals, signs

A **police officer's** directions override everything – traffic lights, signs, markings and the general rules [[src:tf|2:3]]. A **traffic light with a steady light** overrides give-way and stop signs [[src:tf|2:2]]. If the lights are switched off or only flash yellow, the signs and the general rules apply. See [[lesson:signs-basics]].

## The traffic light

[[signs:SIG1,SIG2,SIG3,SIG4,SIG5]]

The ordinary traffic light (*flerfärgssignal*) has three lights: red, yellow and green [[src:vmf|3:3]]. It stands on the right side of the road and is repeated just before, in or after a junction [[src:vmf|3:4]]. The lights come in this order [[src:vmf|3:5]]:

**red → red + yellow → green → yellow → red**

| Light | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[[sign:SIG1|Red]]** | **Stop.** Do not pass the stop line – or, if there is none, the signal [[src:vmf|3:6]]. |
| **[[sign:SIG2|Red + yellow]]** | Green is coming **immediately**. Otherwise it means the same as red: you may **not** start yet [[src:vmf|3:6]]. |
| **[[sign:SIG3|Green]]** | You may drive on [[src:vmf|3:6]]. |
| **[[sign:SIG4|Yellow]]** | **Stop.** Do not pass the stop line or signal – **unless** you were so close when it changed from green to yellow that you cannot stop without danger [[src:vmf|3:6]]. |
| **[[sign:SIG5|Flashing yellow]]** | Take **special care**. Only shown when the signal is out of order or temporarily not working [[src:vmf|3:5]] [[src:vmf|3:6]]. |

::: key Yellow means stop
Many people think yellow means "hurry". It does not. Yellow means **stop** – the only exception is when stopping would be **dangerous** because you are already too close. Look ahead: a green light that has been green for a long time may soon change.
:::

::: warning Green does not mean "go without looking"
Green only means you **may** drive on. When you turn, you must still let oncoming traffic pass [[src:tf|3:24]], and after turning you must drive slowly and give way to pedestrians who are crossing correctly – even though you have green [[src:tf|3:60]]. Do not drive into the junction if you might have to stop in it and block the crossing traffic [[src:tf|3:20]].
:::

### Arrows

- A **green arrow** means the signal applies to the direction of the arrow. You may drive that way **even if another light is shown at the same time** – for example a green left arrow next to a red main light [[src:vmf|3:6]].
- A **red or yellow outline arrow** applies only to the direction(s) of the arrow [[src:vmf|3:6]].
- Over a lane, arrow symbols on the black background screen show which directions the signal is for [[src:vmf|3:3]].

### Cycle and pedestrian lights

[[signs:SIG6,SIG7]]

A **cycle signal** has a white bicycle on a blue background above the red light. For cyclists and class II moped riders, the cycle signal applies **before** the vehicle signal [[src:vmf|3:2]] [[src:vmf|3:3]].

A **pedestrian signal** has a red standing person and a green walking person. It can tick: **slow ticking** = red, **fast ticking** = green. A **flashing green** person means red is coming soon. A pedestrian still on the road when it turns red should continue to the other side [[src:vmf|3:8]] – so expect people on the crossing even after their light has changed.

## Signals for buses and trams

[[signs:SIG8,SIG9,SIG10,SIG11]]

These signals have **white** symbols: **S**, a horizontal bar and a vertical bar or arrow [[src:vmf|3:10]]. They apply to vehicles in scheduled services and trams – and to everyone who uses a bus lane where the signal stands [[src:vmf|3:9]]. **S** means stop, a **vertical bar or arrow** means go, a **horizontal bar** means stop unless you cannot stop safely [[src:vmf|3:12]]. If you drive an ordinary car in an ordinary lane, these signals are not for you – follow your own traffic light.

## Lane signals

[[signs:SIG12,SIG13,SIG14]]

A lane signal hangs over a lane and applies to the drivers in that lane [[src:vmf|3:13]]:

- **[[sign:SIG12|Red cross]]** – the lane, and the shoulder beside it, may **not** be used [[src:vmf|3:15]].
- **[[sign:SIG13|Yellow arrow]]** – **change lane** in the direction of the arrow [[src:vmf|3:15]].
- **[[sign:SIG14|Green arrow]]** pointing down – the lane may be used [[src:vmf|3:15]].

## Flashing red, steady red, flashing yellow

[[signs:SIG16,SIG17,SIG18]]

- **[[sign:SIG16|Two red lights flashing in turn]]** – used at opening bridges, fire and ambulance stations, airfields, road works, tunnels and similar places. It means **stop** – at the stop line or before the signal. A ringing sound can go with it and means the same [[src:vmf|3:19]] [[src:vmf|3:20]].
- **[[sign:SIG17|Steady red light]]** – the road is **completely closed** [[src:vmf|3:21]].
- **[[sign:SIG18|Flashing yellow light]]** – the road is partly closed, or it is there to make a sign or device easier to see [[src:vmf|3:21]].

Level-crossing lights and bells are covered in [[lesson:railway-crossings]].

## Police signals

A police officer – and others appointed by an authority to direct or check traffic, such as a vehicle inspector – gives directions with signs [[src:vmf|7:1]]. You **must obey** them [[src:tf|2:3]], and the driver of a motor vehicle must stop when such a person signals to stop for a check [[src:tf|4:1]]. Their authority must be clear from their uniform or another special sign [[src:tf|2:4]].

[[signs:P1,P2,P3,P4,P5]]

- **[[sign:P1|P1 stop – arm stretched out]]** – **stop** for traffic coming from the **front or behind**. For traffic coming from the **side**, the road is free – you may drive. It applies as long as the officer faces the same way [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P2|P2 stop – raised hand]]** – stop for the road user the **palm faces** [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P3|P3 stop – lamp]]** – stop for the road user the **lamp** is pointed at; used in darkness and poor visibility [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P4|P4 drive on]]** – a waving movement in the direction of travel [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P5|P5 slow down]]** – reduce speed [[src:vmf|7:2]].

::: tip P1 in plain words
Think of the officer's body as a wall. If you see the officer's **front or back**, the wall is in your way – **stop**. If you see the officer's **side**, the road along the outstretched arms is open – **go**.
:::

[[signs:P6,P7,P8,P9,P10,P11]]

- **[[sign:P6|P6 control point]]** and **[[sign:P7|P7 control point ahead]]** – drive in for a check. If a vehicle symbol is shown, only those vehicles must drive in [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P8|P8]]** and **[[sign:P9|P9]]** – from a police vehicle: traffic **behind** (P8) or **oncoming** (P9) must slow down [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P10|P10]]** – the vehicle **behind** the police car must **follow it and stop** when it stops [[src:vmf|7:2]].
- **[[sign:P11|P11]]** – **blue and red lights flashing in turn** from a police vehicle behind you: the vehicle **in front** must **pull over to the side and stop** [[src:vmf|7:2]].

## Guards at road works

[[signs:V1,V2,V3]]

At road works, or when a level-crossing barrier does not work, a **guard** (*vakt*) can direct traffic [[src:vmf|1:7]]. The guard wears special identification on the front and back ([[sign:V3|V3]]) [[src:vmf|5:2]].

- **[[sign:V1|V1 stop]]** – stop for the road user the guard faces. In darkness it is given with a **red lamp**. It can also be given with a sign saying **STOPP VAKT** [[src:vmf|5:2]].
- **[[sign:V2|V2 drive on]]** – a waving movement in the direction of travel [[src:vmf|5:2]].

You must follow a guard's signs, just as you follow signs and signals [[src:tf|2:2]].

## Marker boards, barriers and arrows

[[signs:X1,X2-1,X3-1,X3-2,X3-3,X4-1,X5-1]]

- **[[sign:X1|X1 marker arrow]]** – you must turn **sharply** in the direction of the yellow arrows, for example at a sharp bend [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X2|X2 obstacle marker board]]** – the road is narrowed by an obstacle, or closed [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X3|X3 side obstacle marker]]** – marks the edge of the road or separates directions at complicated places. **Pass on the side the yellow fields slope down towards.** With **horizontal** fields (a temporary version) you may pass on both sides [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X4|X4 exit marker]]** – the point where an exit with a deceleration lane separates from the main carriageway [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X5|X5 yellow light arrow]]** – pass on the side the arrow shows; it can flash [[src:vmf|5:1]].

When X1, X2 or X3 are used at **road works** or for another temporary reason, they have a **red** background instead of blue [[src:vmf|5:1]].

[[signs:X6-1,X6-3,X7,X8,X9,X10]]

- **[[sign:X6|X6 special warning device]]** – a temporary obstacle; the text shows why, for example *Olycka* (accident), *Kontroll* (check), *Polis* or *Bärgning* (vehicle recovery) [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X7|X7 road barrier]]** – the road is wholly or partly closed [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X8|X8 temporary closure]]** – a cross over a sign: the direction shown on that sign does **not** apply for the moment [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X9|X9 roundabout centre marker]]** – the centre of a roundabout, which you drive around [[src:vmf|5:1]].
- **[[sign:X10|X10 post marker]]** – makes a sign post easier to see [[src:vmf|5:1]].
