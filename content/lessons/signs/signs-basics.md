## The sign groups

The Road Signs Ordinance (*vägmärkesförordningen*, VMF) sorts every road sign into a group with a letter [[src:vmf|2:1]]. The letter is the start of the sign's official code – **C31** is sign number 31 in group C. You will meet these codes all through this site and in the theory test.

| Group | English | Swedish | How it usually looks | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Warning signs | *varningsmärken* | Triangle with the point up, yellow with a red border | [[sign:A1-1|A1]] |
| **B** | Priority signs | *väjningspliktsmärken* | Several shapes – best known are the triangle with the point down, the octagon and the diamond | [[sign:B1|B1]] [[sign:B2|B2]] [[sign:B4|B4]] |
| **C** | Prohibitory signs | *förbudsmärken* | Round and red-edged – usually yellow inside, blue inside for the no-parking and no-stopping signs | [[sign:C31-5|C31]] [[sign:C35|C35]] |
| **D** | Mandatory signs | *påbudsmärken* | Round and blue with a white symbol | [[sign:D1|D1]] |
| **E** | Information signs | *anvisningsmärken* | Rectangle or square, mostly blue | [[sign:E19|E19]] |
| **F** | Direction signs | *lokaliseringsmärken för vägvisning* | Rectangle; the colour shows the type of road | [[sign:F1|F1]] |
| **G** | Public facility signs | *lokaliseringsmärken för upplysning om allmänna inrättningar* | Blue with a white square | [[sign:G1|G1]] |
| **H** | Service signs | *lokaliseringsmärken för upplysning om serviceanläggningar* | Blue with a white square | [[sign:H3|H3]] |
| **I** | Tourist signs | *lokaliseringsmärken för turistiskt intressanta mål* | Brown | [[sign:I1|I1]] |
| **J** | Notice signs | *upplysningsmärken* | Usually yellow with black text | [[sign:J2|J2]] |

Beside the signs there are **additional panels** (*tilläggstavlor*, code T), **road markings** (code M), **traffic signals**, other devices such as marker boards (X), signs given by a **guard** (V), level crossing devices (Y) and **signals from the police** (P). Each has its own lesson in this module.

::: tip Learn the shapes first
Shape and colour tell you the kind of message before you can read the sign. A **triangle** warns. A **red ring** forbids. A **blue circle** orders. A **blue rectangle** informs. That is enough to react correctly at speed, and then you read the details.
:::

## Signs stand on the right

::: law vmf 2:3
> Vägmärken är uppsatta på höger sida av vägen, banan eller körfältet om inte annat framgår av denna förordning eller av föreskrifter som meddelats med stöd av denna förordning.
>
> Förbudsmärken och påbudsmärken som är uppsatta över körbanan gäller endast det körfält som de har placerats över. Märke C31, hastighetsbegränsning, gäller dock i förekommande fall även vägrenen intill det körfält märket placerats över.

**In English:** "Road signs are placed on the right side of the road, path or lane, unless this ordinance or regulations issued under it say otherwise. Prohibitory and mandatory signs placed over the carriageway apply only to the lane they are placed over. Sign C31, speed limit, does however also apply to the hard shoulder next to the lane the sign is placed over."
:::

So a speed limit sign hanging over the left lane applies to the left lane only. Where a road has several lanes in the same direction, warning, priority, prohibitory and mandatory signs are also put up on the **left** side or on the central reserve, so you can see them even when a truck hides the right side [[src:tsfs-2019-74|1:14]].

## Where a sign starts and stops applying

This is one of the most tested ideas. The answer depends on the group.

### Prohibitory signs (C)

::: law vmf 2:7
> En anvisning om förbud genom ett förbudsmärke gäller, om inte annat anges nedan eller i 8 §, från den plats där märket satts upp antingen till nästa korsning med en annan väg än sådan som avses i 3 kap. 21 § andra stycket trafikförordningen (1998:1276) eller till den plats dessförinnan där ett motsvarande märke med tilläggstavla T11, utsträckning, med nedåtriktad pil eller ett slutmärke har satts upp.

**In English:** "A prohibition shown by a prohibitory sign applies, unless otherwise stated below or in section 8, from the place where the sign stands either to the next junction with another road – other than the kind of exit described in chapter 3, section 21, second paragraph of the Road Traffic Ordinance – or to an earlier place where the same sign with additional panel T11 (extent) with a downward arrow, or an end sign, has been put up."
:::

In plain words: **a prohibition starts at the sign and normally ends at the next junction.** If it continues after the junction, the sign is repeated. Exits from car parks, petrol stations, private property, small tracks, cycle tracks and similar places do **not** count as junctions here [[src:tf|3:21]].

Some prohibitory signs have their own rules that override this:

- [[sign:C31-5|C31 speed limit]] applies until a **new speed limit**, the end of a temporary limit ([[sign:C32|C32]]), a pedestrian street ([[sign:E7|E7]]) or a walking-speed area ([[sign:E9|E9]]) [[src:vmf|2:8]]. A junction does not end it.
- [[sign:C27|C27 no overtaking]] applies until sign [[sign:C28|C28]], end of no overtaking [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- The parking signs C35–C39 apply on **the side of the road where they stand** [[src:vmf|2:8]].

::: key A sign together with the built-up area sign
If a prohibitory sign is put up together with sign [[sign:E5|E5 built-up area]], the prohibition applies in **the whole built-up area**, unless something else is shown for a certain road. It does not have to be repeated inside the area [[src:vmf|2:7]].
:::

A prohibition aimed at **vehicles** does not apply to people who count as pedestrians – for example someone pushing a bicycle or moped – unless a panel says so [[src:vmf|2:7]] [[src:tf|1:4]].

### Mandatory signs (D)

::: law vmf 2:9
> Påbudsmärken som anger att ett visst slag av trafik är påbjuden anger även förbud mot annan trafik, om inte annat är föreskrivet.

**In English:** "Mandatory signs that show that a certain kind of traffic is mandatory also show a prohibition against other traffic, unless otherwise prescribed."
:::

A mandatory sign also starts at the sign and applies to the **next junction** (or to an end sign or a T11 panel with a downward arrow), exactly like a prohibitory sign [[src:vmf|2:9]]. So [[sign:D4|D4 cycle track]] does not only say that cyclists must use the track – it also says that cars may not.

### Information signs (E)

Information signs tell you about conditions on a stretch of road. They apply **from the sign to an end sign** [[src:vmf|2:11]]. For example, [[sign:E1|E1 motorway]] applies until [[sign:E2|E2 end of motorway]], and [[sign:E5|E5 built-up area]] until [[sign:E6|E6 end of built-up area]].

### Warning signs (A)

Warning signs are placed **before** the danger, at the distance that works best by day and by night [[src:vmf|2:4]]. The [[lesson:warning-signs]] lesson gives the exact distances.

## Additional panels

An additional panel (*tilläggstavla*) under a sign gives extra information about the sign above it – for example the distance, the time, or the vehicles it applies to [[src:vmf|2:29]].

::: law vmf 2:2
> Om det finns flera tilläggstavlor under ett vägmärke gäller anvisningen på var och en av dessa som en kompletterande anvisning till märket. Innehåller en tilläggstavla flera anvisningar gäller dessa gemensamt som en kompletterande anvisning till märket.
>
> En tilläggstavla under två eller flera vägmärken som satts upp direkt över varandra avser samtliga dessa märken.

**In English:** "If there are several additional panels under a road sign, each of them applies as a separate addition to the sign. If one panel holds several instructions, they apply together as one addition to the sign. A panel under two or more signs placed directly above each other applies to all of those signs."
:::

A panel normally has the same colours as the sign above it. Under a **no-parking** or **no-stopping** sign, the panel is **yellow with black text** [[src:vmf|2:29]]. See [[lesson:additional-panels]].

## Lit and electronic signs

Signs can be lit. Then the text and symbols may be **white on a dark background**, but the border, slash and other red parts are always shown in **red** [[src:vmf|1:4]]. Electronic signs over large roads work like this, and they mean exactly the same as the painted versions.

## Which instruction wins?

Sometimes a sign says one thing and the general traffic rule says another, or a police officer waves you through a red light. The Road Traffic Ordinance (*trafikförordningen*, TF) sets the order.

::: law tf 2:2
> En trafikant ska följa anvisningar för trafiken som meddelas genom ett vägmärke, en vägmarkering, en trafiksignal, en ljussignal eller ett sådant tecken av en vakt som avses i 5 kap. 2 § vägmärkesförordningen (2007:90). Om en anvisning innebär en avvikelse från en trafikregel, gäller anvisningen framför regeln.
>
> En anvisning genom fast sken i en trafiksignal gäller framför en anvisning om stopplikt eller väjningsplikt som meddelas genom ett vägmärke.
>
> En särskild anvisning för cyklist, förare av moped klass II eller gående gäller för sådan trafikant framför andra anvisningar.

**In English:** "A road user must follow the instructions given by a road sign, a road marking, a traffic signal, a light signal or a sign from a guard. If an instruction differs from a traffic rule, the instruction applies before the rule. An instruction given by a steady light in a traffic signal applies before a stop or give-way instruction given by a road sign. A special instruction for cyclists, riders of class II mopeds or pedestrians applies to them before other instructions."
:::

::: law tf 2:3
> En trafikant ska lyda en polismans anvisning för trafiken. En anvisning som ges av en polisman gäller framför trafikreglerna och sådana anvisningar som avses i 2 §.

**In English:** "A road user must obey a police officer's directions for traffic. A direction given by a police officer applies before the traffic rules and before the instructions mentioned in section 2."
:::

::: steps
1. **A police officer** – and others the authorities appoint to direct traffic, such as a vehicle inspector – overrides everything else [[src:tf|2:3]].
2. **A traffic signal with a steady light** overrides a give-way or stop sign [[src:tf|2:2]]. When the signal is switched off or only flashes yellow, the signs apply.
3. **Signs, markings, signals and a guard's signs** override the **general traffic rules**, such as the right-hand rule [[src:tf|2:2]].
4. **The general traffic rules** apply when nothing else says otherwise.
:::

::: example A sign beats the general rule
In a built-up area the general rule is 50 km/h [[src:tf|3:17]]. If a [[sign:C31-4|40 km/h sign]] stands there, you may drive at most 40 km/h, because the sign applies before the rule.
:::
