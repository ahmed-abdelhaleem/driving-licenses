## How mandatory signs work

Mandatory signs (*påbudsmärken*) are **round and blue with white symbols**. They tell you what you **must** do. Like a prohibition, the order starts at the sign and normally applies to the **next junction**, or to an end sign [[src:vmf|2:9]].

::: key A mandatory sign forbids too
A sign that says a path or lane is **for a certain kind of traffic** also means that **all other traffic is forbidden there** [[src:vmf|2:9]]. The cycle-track sign does not only invite cyclists – it bans cars. Other traffic may only **cross** such a path or lane [[src:tf|3:6]].
:::

## Which way you must drive

[[signs:D1-1,D2-1,D3]]

- **[[sign:D1|D1 mandatory direction]]** – vehicles may only go in the direction of the arrow – or, with two arrows, in either of those directions [[src:vmf|2:10]]. You often see it on a traffic island at a junction or at the entry to a one-way road.
- **[[sign:D2|D2 pass this side]]** – vehicles may only pass the sign on the side the arrow shows (or either side, with two arrows) [[src:vmf|2:10]]. It is common on traffic islands and central reserves [[src:tsfs-2019-74|5:6]].
- **[[sign:D3|D3 roundabout]]** – marks a [[term:cirkulationsplats|roundabout]]. It is always put up together with a give-way (B1) or stop (B2) sign [[src:tsfs-2019-74|5:8]].

::: law tf 3:22
> En förare som kör in i en cirkulationsplats har väjningsplikt mot varje fordon som befinner sig i cirkulationen.

**In English:** "A driver entering a roundabout must give way to every vehicle that is already in the roundabout."
:::

In a roundabout you drive **anticlockwise** around the central island [[src:tf|3:13]]. The [[lesson:turning-and-lanes]] lesson explains lanes, signalling and position in roundabouts.

::: note Islands without a sign
Without a D2 sign, you pass an island in the middle of a two-way road on the **right**. If the island is to the right of the centre of the road, or on a one-way road, you may pass on either side unless a sign says otherwise [[src:tf|3:13]].
:::

## Paths for cyclists, pedestrians and riders

[[signs:D4,D5,D6,D7-1,D8,D9]]

- **[[sign:D4|D4 cycle track]]** – for cyclists and riders of class II mopeds. If class II mopeds are not allowed, a panel says so [[src:vmf|2:10]].
- **[[sign:D5|D5 footpath]]** – for **pedestrians only** [[src:vmf|2:10]].
- **[[sign:D6|D6 shared path]]** – one path shared by pedestrians and cyclists [[src:vmf|2:10]].
- **[[sign:D7|D7 separated paths]]** – the path is divided by a line or a strip into one part for pedestrians and one for cyclists. The side each symbol is on shows which part is which [[src:vmf|2:10]].
- **[[sign:D8|D8 bridle path]]** – for horse riders; pedestrians may use it too [[src:vmf|2:10]].
- **[[sign:D9|D9 route for off-road vehicles]]** – for example snowmobiles; pedestrians may use it too [[src:vmf|2:10]].

As a driver you may only **cross** these paths, for example to reach a property. When you do, you must give way: on a cycle track, cyclists and class II moped riders have priority over other drivers [[src:tf|3:59]], and on a footpath you must give way to pedestrians [[src:tf|3:58]]. Before you enter the road from a path or property, you also give way to the traffic on the road [[src:tf|3:21]].

## Bus lanes

[[signs:D10,D11]]

**[[sign:D10|D10]]** marks a lane or carriageway for **vehicles in scheduled services** (*linjetrafik* – mostly buses) and some others [[src:vmf|2:10]].

::: law tf 8:2
> I ett körfält eller en körbana för fordon i linjetrafik m.fl. får endast fordon i linjetrafik föras samt, om körfältet eller körbanan är beläget till höger i färdriktningen, cykel och moped klass II.

**In English:** "In a lane or carriageway for vehicles in scheduled services etc., only vehicles in scheduled services may be driven – and, if the lane or carriageway is on the right in the direction of travel, bicycles and class II mopeds."
:::

- A **panel** under the sign may let other vehicles in – for example taxis – or give the times the bus lane applies [[src:vmf|2:10]].
- Other drivers may only **cross** the lane, for example to turn [[src:tf|3:10]].
- You may not stop or park in a bus lane [[src:tf|3:53]]. The road is often marked with the word **BUSS** ([[sign:M28|M28]]) and the bus-lane line ([[sign:M6|M6]]) [[src:vmf|4:4]] [[src:vmf|4:9]].

::: only motorcycle
**For riders:** a motorcycle is not a bus. Unless a panel allows motorcycles, you may not ride in a bus lane – only cross it.
:::

**[[sign:D11|D11]]** marks the end of a mandatory path, lane, road or route shown with D4–D10. It is not needed where the end is obvious anyway [[src:vmf|2:10]].

## Dangerous goods

[[signs:D12]]

**[[sign:D12|D12]]** shows the direction that vehicles carrying **dangerous goods** (marked with orange plates) must take. A letter on the sign means the order only applies to vehicles that may not be driven through road tunnels of that category [[src:vmf|2:10]]. It matters mostly to professional drivers, but you should recognise it.
