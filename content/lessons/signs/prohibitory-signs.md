## How prohibitory signs work

Prohibitory signs (*förbudsmärken*) are **round with a red edge**. Most are yellow inside; the no-parking and no-stopping signs are blue inside. A prohibition starts at the sign and normally ends at the **next junction**, unless the sign has its own rule [[src:vmf|2:7]]. The exceptions to remember – speed limits, no-overtaking and the parking signs – are explained below and in [[lesson:signs-basics]].

## Bans on traffic

[[signs:C1,C2,C3,C4,C5,C6,C7,C8]]

- **[[sign:C1|C1 no entry]]** – you may not drive **into** the road from this direction. You see it at the far end of a **one-way street**, where the traffic comes towards you [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:3]].
- **[[sign:C2|C2 no vehicles]]** – no vehicles at all, in **either direction** [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C3|C3 no motor vehicles]]** – except class II mopeds. A panel says if class II mopeds are banned too.
- **[[sign:C4|C4 no motor vehicles with more than two wheels]]** – cars are banned, but motorcycles and two-wheeled mopeds may pass.
- **[[sign:C5|C5 no motorcycles or class I mopeds]]**.
- **[[sign:C6|C6 no motor vehicles with a trailer]]** – applies to trailers other than semi-trailers and light trailers (*släpkärra*). If those are banned too, or only combinations over a certain weight, a panel says so [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C7|C7 no heavy trucks]]** and **[[sign:C8|C8 no tractors or class II motor implements]]**.

::: key C1 or C2?
**C1** is red with a horizontal bar. It stops traffic going **in** from your side – traffic from the other side may come out. **C2** is a red ring with a plain inside. It closes the road to vehicles **in both directions**.
:::

[[signs:C9,C10,C11,C12,C13,C14,C15,C44]]

- **[[sign:C9|C9 no vehicles carrying dangerous goods]]** – vehicles that must have orange plates for dangerous goods. With panel T23 it shows a **tunnel category** [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C10|C10 no cycles or class II mopeds]]**, **[[sign:C11|C11 no class II mopeds]]**, **[[sign:C12|C12 no horse-drawn vehicles]]**, **[[sign:C13|C13 no off-road vehicles]]**, **[[sign:C14|C14 no horse riding]]**, **[[sign:C15|C15 no pedestrians]]**.
- **[[sign:C44|C44 no motor vehicles with studded tyres]]** – except class II mopeds [[src:vmf|2:8]]. Used on some city streets.

A ban aimed at **vehicles** does not apply to someone who walks beside a bicycle or moped, because they count as a pedestrian – unless a panel says otherwise [[src:vmf|2:7]] [[src:tf|1:4]].

## Size, weight and distance limits

[[signs:C16,C17,C18,C19,C20,C21,C22,C23,C24,C46]]

- **[[sign:C16|C16 width limit]]**, **[[sign:C17|C17 height limit]]** and **[[sign:C18|C18 length limit]]** give the maximum width, height or length allowed. C17 is used when the free height is lower than **4.5 metres** [[src:vmf|2:8]]. Remember a roof box, a bike on the roof or a high load.
- **[[sign:C19|C19 minimum distance]]** – you may not drive closer than the distance shown to the **motor vehicle in front** going the same way [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C20|C20]]** and **[[sign:C21|C21 weight limits]]** – C20 limits the gross weight of each vehicle; C21 also limits a **vehicle combination**, such as a car with a trailer [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C22|C22 bearing capacity class]]**, **[[sign:C23|C23 axle load]]**, **[[sign:C24|C24 bogie load]]** and **[[sign:C46|C46 triple axle load]]** – mostly matter for heavy vehicles.

::: law vtdl 2
> Bruttovikt på fordon: Den sammanlagda statiska vikt som samtliga hjul, band eller medar på ett fordon vid ett visst tillfälle för över till vägbanan.

**In English:** "Gross weight of a vehicle: the total static weight that all the wheels, tracks or runners of a vehicle put on the road surface at a given moment."
:::

So the weight signs are about what the vehicle **actually weighs right now** – people and load included – not about the highest weight it is registered for (the [[term:totalvikt|total weight]]).

## Turning and overtaking

[[signs:C25-1,C25-2,C26,C27,C28,C29,C30]]

- **[[sign:C25|C25 no turning]]** – the symbol shows the turn that is banned. It applies in the junction the sign stands in or before [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C26|C26 no U-turns]]** – also applies in the junction it stands in or before [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C27|C27 no overtaking]]** – see below.
- **[[sign:C29|C29 no overtaking by heavy trucks]]** – only drivers of heavy trucks are banned. Ends at [[sign:C30|C30]].

::: law vmf 2:8 C27
> Märket anger förbud mot att köra om andra motordrivna fordon än tvåhjuliga mopeder och tvåhjuliga motorcyklar utan sidvagn.
>
> Förbudet gäller på den väg märket är uppsatt och till den plats där märke C28, slut på förbud mot omkörning, är uppsatt.

**In English:** "The sign prohibits overtaking motor vehicles other than two-wheeled mopeds and two-wheeled motorcycles without a sidecar. The ban applies on the road where the sign is placed, up to the place where sign C28, end of no overtaking, is placed."
:::

::: key What may you overtake under C27?
- **Yes** (if it is safe): bicycles, two-wheeled mopeds, two-wheeled motorcycles without a sidecar, horse riders and horse-drawn vehicles – they are not banned.
- **No:** cars, trucks, buses, tractors and all other motor vehicles.
- The ban **does not end at a junction** – only at **C28** [[src:vmf|2:8]]. The sign stands on **both sides** of the road [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:23]].
:::

::: only motorcycle
**For riders:** the ban applies to you too. On a motorcycle you may not overtake a car under C27 – but cars may overtake you, because you ride a two-wheeled motorcycle without a sidecar.
:::

## Speed limits

[[signs:C31-4,C31-5,C31-7,C31-9,C32-5]]

**[[sign:C31|C31 speed limit]]** forbids driving faster than the speed shown [[src:vmf|2:8]]. Unlike most prohibitory signs it does **not** end at a junction. It applies until:

- a sign with a **different speed limit**,
- **[[sign:C32|C32 end of temporary speed limit]]** – after it, the limit that applied before returns,
- **[[sign:E7|E7 pedestrian street]]** or **[[sign:E9|E9 walking-speed area]]** [[src:vmf|2:8]].

It is placed on **both sides** of the road where a limit begins. When the limit drops by **more than 20 km/h at once**, the sign is either shown in advance with a distance panel, or made large or very large so you notice it in time [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:25]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:26]]. The rules for speed are explained in [[lesson:speed-limits]].

## Stop for customs, checks and more

[[signs:C33,C34-3,C34-4,C34-5]]

- **[[sign:C33|C33 stop at customs]]** – stop for customs formalities, at the stop line or else at the sign [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C34|C34 stop for the reason given]]** – stop for the reason written under the bar: **FÄRJA** (ferry), **KONTROLL** (check), **VAKT** (guard) [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:31]]. If it stands before a traffic signal, you must stop when the signal shows red [[src:vmf|2:8]].

## Stopping and parking signs

First, two words that the law defines exactly:

::: law vtdf 2
> Parkering: En uppställning av ett fordon med eller utan förare av någon annan anledning än som 1. föranleds av trafikförhållandena, 2. sker för att undvika fara, eller 3. sker för på- eller avstigning eller på- eller avlastning av gods.
>
> Stannande: Annat stillastående med ett fordon än som 1. sker för att undvika fara, 2. föranleds av trafikförhållandena, eller 3. utgör parkering.

**In English:** "Parking: leaving a vehicle standing, with or without a driver, for any reason other than 1. the traffic situation, 2. avoiding danger, or 3. people getting in or out, or loading or unloading goods. Stopping: standing still with a vehicle for a reason other than 1. avoiding danger, 2. the traffic situation, or 3. parking."
:::

In plain words: [[term:stannande|stopping]] is a short stop **to let people in or out or to load or unload**. Anything else – even sitting in the car with the engine running while you wait for a friend – is [[term:parkering|parking]]. Standing still in a queue or at a red light is neither.

[[signs:C35,C36,C37,C38,C39]]

- **[[sign:C35|C35 no parking]]** – a blue sign with a red ring and **one** red slash. You may stop (to let someone out, or to load) but not park. It applies on **the side of the road where it stands**, from the sign to the next junction, to another stopping or parking sign, or to a matching sign with a downward arrow panel [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C36|C36 no parking on odd dates]]** (1st, 3rd, 5th …) and **[[sign:C37|C37 no parking on even dates]]** (2nd, 4th, 6th …).
- **[[sign:C38|C38 date parking]]** – on **even** dates you may not park on the side with **even** house numbers, and on **odd** dates not on the side with **odd** house numbers [[src:vmf|2:8]].
- **[[sign:C39|C39 no stopping or parking]]** – a red **cross**. You may not even stop to let a passenger out [[src:vmf|2:8]]. Only standing still because of the traffic, or to avoid danger, is allowed.

::: tip Read the panels
Under these signs the panels are **yellow with black text** [[src:vmf|2:29]]. A panel such as **8–18** means the ban applies only at those times. Without a time panel, the ban applies **all the time**. See [[lesson:additional-panels]].
:::

[[signs:C40,C41,C42,C43,C45]]

- **[[sign:C40|C40 special-purpose place]]** – reserved for a purpose. *Lastplats* means loading or unloading **heavy or bulky goods** [[src:vmf|2:8]]. Other purposes can be *Taxiplats* (taxis), *Bokbussplats* (mobile library), *Skolskjutsplats* (school transport), *Ambulansplats* (ambulances) and *På- och avstigningsplats* (getting in and out) [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:37]]. You may only park or stop there for that purpose – but you may stop briefly to let people in or out if it does not hinder the purpose [[src:tf|3:54]]. [[sign:C41|C41]] marks the end.
- **[[sign:C42|C42 turning place]]** – a place to turn vehicles around, where you may **not park**. If the no-stopping cross is shown in the sign, you may not stop either [[src:vmf|2:8]]. [[sign:C43|C43]] marks the end.
- **[[sign:C45|C45 special rules for stopping and parking]]** – a panel with a small prohibitory sign and times inside it, placed under another sign. It adds a ban or makes an exception [[src:vmf|2:8]].

::: warning A no-parking sign does not make stopping legal everywhere
The parking signs do not give you the right to stop or park where the Road Traffic Ordinance already forbids it – for example within 10 metres before a pedestrian crossing – unless the sign's position or a panel clearly shows that you may [[src:vmf|2:8]] [[src:tf|3:53]]. The general stopping and parking rules are in [[lesson:stopping-and-parking]].
:::
