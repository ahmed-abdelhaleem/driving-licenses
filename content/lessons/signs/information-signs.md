## How information signs work

Information signs (*anvisningsmärken*) tell you about the conditions on a place, a road or a stretch of road [[src:vmf|2:11]]. Most are **blue rectangles or squares**; the motorway and expressway signs are **green**. An information sign applies **from the sign until an end sign** – usually the same sign with a red diagonal line [[src:vmf|2:11]].

Several of these signs switch on a whole set of rules from the Road Traffic Ordinance. Those are the ones to learn best.

## Motorways and expressways

[[signs:E1,E2,E3,E4]]

- **[[sign:E1|E1 motorway]]** (*motorväg*) and **[[sign:E2|E2 end of motorway]]**.
- **[[sign:E3|E3 expressway]]** (*motortrafikled*) and **[[sign:E4|E4 end of expressway]]**.

On both, special rules apply: only motor vehicles that are built for and allowed to go at least 40 km/h; you may only enter and leave at the start, end or slip roads; you may not turn around, reverse, stop or park except at signed parking places and rest areas. On a motorway the limit is **110 km/h** unless signs show otherwise [[src:tf|9:1]] [[src:tf|9:2]]. The [[lesson:motorways]] lesson explains everything.

E1 stands on both sides where the motorway begins and is repeated after each slip road that is not itself a motorway [[src:tsfs-2019-74|6:3]].

## Built-up area

[[signs:E5,E6]]

**[[sign:E5|E5 built-up area]]** (*tättbebyggt område*) shows that you are entering a built-up area; the name of the place can be in the sign. **[[sign:E6|E6]]** shows that it ends [[src:vmf|2:12]].

::: law tf 3:17
> Inom tättbebyggt område får fordon inte föras med högre hastighet än 50 kilometer i timmen.

**In English:** "Within a built-up area vehicles may not be driven faster than 50 kilometres per hour."
:::

So **50 km/h** applies after E5, unless speed limit signs show something else. The municipality may decide on 30 or 40 km/h for the area or parts of it [[src:tf|3:17]]. A prohibitory sign put up together with E5 applies to the whole built-up area [[src:vmf|2:7]].

::: warning The sign is not what makes an area "built-up"
A built-up area is decided in a **local traffic regulation** (*lokal trafikföreskrift*) [[src:tf|10:1]]. The E5 sign tells you where it starts. Do not guess from the houses alone – look for the sign.
:::

## Pedestrian streets, walking-speed areas and cycle streets

[[signs:E7,E8,E9,E10,E33,E34]]

::: law tf 8:1
> På en gågata och i ett gångfartsområde gäller följande.
>
> 1. Fordon får inte föras med högre hastighet än gångfart.
> 2. Fordon får inte parkeras.
> 3. Fordonsförare har väjningsplikt mot gående.

**In English:** "On a pedestrian street and in a walking-speed area the following applies: 1. Vehicles may not be driven faster than walking pace. 2. Vehicles may not be parked. 3. Drivers must give way to pedestrians."
:::

- **[[sign:E7|E7 pedestrian street]]** (*gågata*): the three rules above, **and** motor vehicles may only **cross** it – except when needed for deliveries to shops, transport of goods or residents to an address on the street, guests to a hotel, or sick or disabled people [[src:tf|8:1]].
- **[[sign:E9|E9 walking-speed area]]** (*gångfartsområde*): the three rules above. Here you may drive through, but only at walking pace and giving way to pedestrians [[src:tf|8:1]].
- **[[sign:E33|E33 cycle street]]** (*cykelgata*): at most **30 km/h**; parking only in specially arranged parking places; a driver who enters a cycle street from another road must give way to vehicles on it; and drivers of motor vehicles must adapt their speed to the cyclists [[src:tf|8:1a]].

E8, E10 and E34 show where each one ends. Remember: a speed limit sign (C31) ends at E7 and E9 [[src:vmf|2:8]]. When you **leave** a pedestrian street, a walking-speed area or a cycle street, you must give way to the traffic on the road you enter [[src:tf|3:21]].

## Recommended speeds

[[signs:E11,E12,E13,E14]]

- **[[sign:E11|E11 recommended lower speed]]** – a blue sign with "max" and a speed. It marks a stretch or area where it is suitable to drive **slower than the speed limit**, for example because of speed bumps or a danger warned of with a warning sign [[src:vmf|2:12]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|6:7]]. E12 ends it.
- **[[sign:E13|E13 recommended maximum speed]]** – an **electronic** sign, white numbers on black. It shows that conditions are **temporarily** such that you should not drive faster – for example because of queues or an incident ahead. The speed shown is always lower than the limit [[src:vmf|2:12]]. E14 ends it.

::: key Limit or recommendation?
A **red-edged round sign** (C31) is a **limit** – driving faster is an offence. A **blue or black square sign** (E11, E13) is a **recommendation**. But the law always requires you to adapt your speed to the conditions [[src:tf|3:14]], so ignoring a recommendation that matched the conditions can still be breaking the law.
:::

## Lanes, one-way streets and narrow roads

[[signs:E15,E16,E17,E18]]

- **[[sign:E15|E15 merging lanes]]** – two lanes or carriageways run together into one. The drivers must adapt to the new situation with **mutual consideration** [[src:vmf|2:12]] [[src:tf|3:44]]. If you see that someone wants to move into your lane, you must adjust your speed to make it easier for them [[src:tf|3:43]]. In practice drivers usually take turns, one from each lane – often called the *zip* principle (*blixtlåsprincipen*). The law itself does not set an order; it requires consideration from both sides.
- **[[sign:E16|E16 one-way traffic]]** – vehicle traffic goes only in the direction of the arrow [[src:vmf|2:12]]. It is only used where all vehicle traffic in the other direction is banned [[src:tsfs-2019-74|6:16]]. At the far end you will find [[sign:C1|C1 no entry]].
- **[[sign:E17|E17 dead end]]** – no through road. If cyclists and class II mopeds can get through, a bicycle symbol is shown in the sign [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- **[[sign:E18|E18 passing place]]** – a place on a narrow road where vehicles can meet or let others pass. You may **not park** there [[src:tf|3:55]].

## Parking

[[signs:E19,E20-16,E21,E30]]

- **[[sign:E19|E19 parking]]** – parking is allowed on the side of the road where the sign stands, or in the whole car park. Panels set the conditions: times, fees, a parking disc, a vehicle type [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- **[[sign:E20|E20 zone sign]]** – an area where the rule shown in the sign applies, for example a parking time limit or a ban on heavy trucks. It applies until **[[sign:E21|E21 end of zone]]** [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- **[[sign:E30|E30 special rules for parking]]** – a panel with parking conditions, placed under another sign [[src:vmf|2:12]].

Even where parking is allowed, a vehicle may not stay parked for more than **24 hours in a row** on weekdays (except the day before a Sunday or public holiday) on public streets in built-up areas run by the municipality and on public roads [[src:tf|3:49a]]. The [[lesson:stopping-and-parking]] lesson covers parking in detail.

## Buses and taxis

[[signs:E22,E23]]

- **[[sign:E22|E22 bus stop]]**. If the stop is not marked on the road, you may not stop or park from **20 metres before to 5 metres after** the sign – except briefly to let people in or out, if it does not hinder the buses [[src:tf|3:54]].
- **[[sign:E23|E23 taxi]]** – marks the far end of a taxi rank [[src:vmf|2:12]].

Where the speed limit is **50 km/h or lower**, you must slow down – and stop if needed – to let a bus signalling that it is leaving a bus stop get out [[src:tf|3:45]].

## Cameras, tolls, tunnels and emergencies

[[signs:E24,E25,E26,E27,E28,E29-1]]

- **[[sign:E24|E24 automatic traffic surveillance]]** – a camera may check speed or other rules, or register you for taxes or fees [[src:vmf|2:12]]. It normally stands **40–300 metres before** the camera [[src:tsfs-2019-74|6:30]].
- **[[sign:E25|E25 toll road or charge]]** – a fee or special tax is charged on the road or in the area, for example congestion tax in a city or a bridge fee [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- **[[sign:E26|E26 tunnel]]** – gives the length of the tunnel and sometimes its name. It is always used for tunnels longer than **500 metres**, and repeated every kilometre with the length left [[src:vmf|2:12]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|6:39]].
- **[[sign:E27|E27 emergency lay-by]]** – a place to stop if your vehicle breaks down; it can show an emergency phone or fire extinguisher [[src:vmf|2:12]].
- **[[sign:E28|E28 emergency exit]]** and **[[sign:E29|E29 escape route]]** – in tunnels, for people on foot. E29 shows the direction and distance to the nearest exit and is placed at most 25 metres apart [[src:vmf|2:12]].

## Environmental zones

[[signs:E31-1,E31-2,E31-3,E32]]

**[[sign:E31|E31 environmental zone]]** (*miljözon*) marks an area where only vehicles that meet certain emission requirements may be driven [[src:vmf|2:12]] [[src:tf|4:22]]. **Class 1** applies to heavy trucks and buses, **class 2** to cars, light trucks and light buses, and **class 3** has the strictest rules. The zone applies until **[[sign:E32|E32]]**, the end-of-built-up-area sign E6, or another zone sign [[src:vmf|2:12]]. See [[lesson:environment-and-vehicles]].
