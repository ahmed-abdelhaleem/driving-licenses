## What a panel does

An additional panel (*tilläggstavla*) gives extra instructions to the sign above it [[src:vmf|2:29]]. Apart from arrow panels that can mark parking spaces inside a car park, a panel always belongs to a sign [[src:vmf|2:30]]. Remember three rules from [[lesson:signs-basics]]:

- **Several panels** under one sign each add their own condition to the sign. If **one panel** holds several instructions, they apply **together** [[src:vmf|2:2]].
- A panel under **two signs placed directly above each other** applies to both signs [[src:vmf|2:2]].
- A panel is normally the same colour as the sign; under a **no-parking or no-stopping** sign it is **yellow with black text** [[src:vmf|2:29]].

## Distance and length

[[signs:T1-1,T2-1,T3]]

- **[[sign:T1|T1 length of stretch]]** – the sign applies for this distance from the sign, for example "0–1,2 km" [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T2|T2 distance]]** – the distance **to** what the sign is about, for example a give-way sign "200 m" ahead [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T3|T3 distance to stop]]** – only used under the give-way sign B1. It warns that a **stop** sign comes after the distance shown [[src:vmf|2:30]].

Distances above one kilometre are given in kilometres [[src:tsfs-2019-74|15:5]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|15:6]].

## Times – panel T6

[[signs:T6]]

The time panel is one of the most tested panels. It shows **when** the sign applies [[src:vmf|2:30]].

::: law vmf 2:30 T6
> Svarta eller vita siffror utan parentes avser vardagar utom vardag före sön- och helgdag.
>
> Svarta eller vita siffror inom parentes avser vardag före sön- och helgdag.
>
> Röda siffror avser sön- och helgdag.
>
> Om angivelsen sträcker sig över midnatt gäller tidsperioden efter midnatt påföljande dag.
>
> Anges viss dag eller viss veckodag gäller regleringen denna dag oavsett om dagen är en vardag, söndag eller helgdag.

**In English:** "Black or white numbers without brackets mean weekdays, except a weekday before a Sunday or public holiday. Black or white numbers in brackets mean a weekday before a Sunday or public holiday. Red numbers mean Sundays and public holidays. If the time runs past midnight, the period after midnight applies on the following day. If a certain date or day of the week is given, the rule applies that day whatever kind of day it is."
:::

::: example Reading the panel in the picture
**8 – 17** · **(8 – 14)** · **8 – 13 in red**, under a no-parking sign:

| Day | No parking |
|---|---|
| Monday–Friday (normal weekdays) | 08:00–17:00 |
| A weekday before a Sunday or public holiday – usually **Saturday** | 08:00–14:00 |
| **Sundays and public holidays** | 08:00–13:00 |

Outside those times the ban does not apply.
:::

::: warning No red numbers = no rule on Sundays
If a time panel shows only "8–18" and "(8–15)" – **no red numbers** – the rule it limits does **not** apply on Sundays and public holidays at all. And a sign **without any time panel** applies **all the time**.
:::

## Arrows and directions

[[signs:T11-1,T11-2,T11-3,T12-1]]

**[[sign:T11|T11 extent]]** [[src:vmf|2:30]]:

- arrows pointing **both ways** (horizontal or vertical): the rule applies on **both sides** of the sign,
- an arrow pointing **down**: the rule **ends here**,
- an arrow to the **right or left**: the rule applies in that direction.

**[[sign:T12|T12 direction]]** shows the direction to what the sign is about, for example where a cycle track is [[src:vmf|2:30]].

## Who and what the sign applies to

[[signs:T4,T5,T8-1,T7,T9,T10]]

- **[[sign:T8|T8 vehicle type or road user panel]]** – the sign above applies **only** to the vehicle type or road users shown by the symbol [[src:vmf|2:30]]. A no-parking sign with a truck symbol only bans parking for trucks.
- **[[sign:T5|T5 total weight]]** – the sign applies to vehicles whose **total weight** (the highest weight the vehicle is registered for, see [[term:totalvikt]]) is above the value shown [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T4|T4 free width]]** – the free width of the road [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T7|T7 disabled parking permit]]** – only used under the parking sign E19: the parking is for people with a **parking permit for people with disabilities** [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T9|T9]]** and **[[sign:T10|T10]]** – many people with **impaired sight** or **impaired hearing** are about. They are only used with warning signs or the pedestrian crossing sign [[src:vmf|2:30]] [[src:tsfs-2019-74|15:7]].

## Junction panels

[[signs:T13,T14,T15-1]]

- **[[sign:T13|T13 all-way give way]]** and **[[sign:T14|T14 all-way stop]]** – all drivers entering the junction must give way, or stop [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T15|T15]]** – shows how the roads continue: drivers on the narrow line give way to vehicles on the wide line [[src:vmf|2:30]]. See [[lesson:priority-signs]].

## Parking panels

[[signs:T16,T17,T18,T19,T20,T21-1,T24]]

- **[[sign:T16|T16 fee]]** (*Avgift*) – you must pay as shown on the parking meter or ticket machine, and follow its other conditions [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T17|T17 parking disc]]** (*P-skiva*) – show the time you parked with a parking disc [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T18|T18 time limit]]** – the longest time you may park, for example "2 tim" (2 hours) [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T19|T19 residents]]** (*Boende*) – residents with a special permit may park on special conditions [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T20|T20 parking ticket]]** – parking is free, but you must use a parking ticket [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T21|T21 how to park]]** – shows how vehicles must be placed, for example at an angle [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T24|T24 charging place]]** (*Laddplats*) – under a parking sign: only vehicles that can be **charged externally** for their propulsion may park there [[src:vmf|2:30]]. Other vehicles may only stop briefly to let people in or out [[src:tf|3:54]].

::: law tf 3:49a
> Om en parkeringsskiva eller motsvarande används, ska tiden på denna ställas in på den närmast följande halvtimmen räknat från den tidpunkt då uppställningen påbörjades.

**In English:** "If a parking disc or similar is used, the time on it must be set to the next half hour after the time the vehicle was parked."
:::

So if you park at **10:10**, set the disc to **10:30**. If you park **before** a time limit starts and will stay after it starts, set the disc to the time the limit begins. Put the disc at the front, inside the vehicle, where it can be read from outside. You do not need a disc if you only park while no time limit applies [[src:tf|3:49a]].

## Other panels

[[signs:T22,T23,T25]]

- **[[sign:T22|T22 text]]** – a written instruction that no other panel can give [[src:vmf|2:30]], for example *Gäller även lätt lastbil* (also applies to light trucks) under the no-heavy-trucks sign [[src:tsfs-2019-74|4:4]].
- **[[sign:T23|T23 tunnel category]]** – used with sign C9 for tunnels where dangerous goods are restricted [[src:vmf|2:30]].
- **[[sign:T25|T25 open 24 hours]]** – under the café or restaurant sign [[src:vmf|2:30]].

## Putting it together

::: example A parking sign with one combined panel
Under a **P sign** (E19) there is **one** panel showing a **parking disc** symbol (T17), **"2 tim"** (T18) and **"8–18 (8–15)"** (T6). Because the instructions are on the same panel, they apply **together** [[src:vmf|2:2]].

**Meaning:** On normal weekdays 08:00–18:00, and on days before a Sunday or public holiday (usually Saturdays) 08:00–15:00, you may park for **at most 2 hours** and must show your arrival time with a **parking disc**. At other times – and all day on Sundays and holidays, since there are no red numbers – the time limit and the disc do not apply. On public streets the general rule of at most 24 hours in a row on weekdays still applies [[src:tf|3:49a]].
:::
