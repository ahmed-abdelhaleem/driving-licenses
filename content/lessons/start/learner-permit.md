## What the permit is

A [[term:korkortstillstand|learner's permit]] (in Swedish *körkortstillstånd*) is a decision from Transportstyrelsen, the Swedish Transport Agency. It says that you meet the **health requirements** and the **personal requirements** for having a licence. It is not a licence: it does not let you drive alone.

::: law kkl 1:1
> … körkortstillstånd, varmed avses ett beslut om att sökanden uppfyller medicinska och personliga krav för att körkort eller traktorkort ska få utfärdas …

**In English:** "… a learner's permit, which means a decision that the applicant meets the medical and personal requirements for a driving licence or tractor licence to be issued …"
:::

You need a valid permit for two things:

- to **practise driving or riding** on the road, with a driving school or privately, and
- to **book and take** the theory test and the driving test. Trafikverket's booking service does not accept a booking without one [[src:tv-theory-b]].

## Which permit to apply for

Permits come in three groups. Cars and motorcycles are both in **group 1**, which covers tractor, AM (moped class I), A1, A2, A, B and BE [[src:ts-permit]]. While the permit is valid you can take any licence in its group. For example, someone who first gets a B licence can later take an A licence on the same permit, as long as it has not expired.

## How to apply

::: steps
1. **Apply online** with Transportstyrelsen's e-service. You log in with an e-ID (*e-legitimation*, for example BankID). If you cannot use an e-ID, you can order a paper form and send it by post to *Transportstyrelsen, 701 97 Örebro*.
2. **Fill in the health declaration** (*hälsodeklaration*). It is a form with questions about your health that you answer yourself. In the e-service you fill it in as part of the application.
3. **Have your eyes tested** by an optician (or a doctor) and send the **eyesight certificate** (*synintyg*). Many opticians send it to Transportstyrelsen digitally. The certificate may not be issued more than [[fact:permit.eyeTestMaxAge]] before you apply. At the eye test you must prove who you are with a valid ID document (or a close relative with valid ID can confirm your identity) [[src:ts-permit]].
4. **Wait for the decision.** When everything is in and there is nothing to investigate, it normally takes [[fact:permit.processing]]. The permit is sent to your digital mailbox if you have one, and you can always see it on *Mina sidor* (My pages) on Transportstyrelsen's website.
:::

::: key Key facts
- Cost to apply: **[[fact:prices.permit]]** [[src:ts-permit]]
- Normal processing time: **[[fact:permit.processing]]**
- Valid for: **[[fact:permit.validity]]**, and it **cannot be extended**
- There is **no minimum age for applying**. The age limits apply to practising and to getting the licence.
:::

## The health requirements

Transportstyrelsen checks your health declaration and eyesight certificate. For cars and motorcycles you need a visual acuity (*synskärpa*) of at least **[[fact:permit.vision]]** when you look with both eyes together. Glasses or contact lenses are allowed [[src:ts-medical]].

Some conditions mean you may have to send a certificate from a doctor, for example diabetes treated with medicine, or epilepsy. Transportstyrelsen tells you which certificate it needs and by when. Many common medicines, such as contraceptive pills, asthma medicine and painkillers you can buy without a prescription, do not need any certificate [[src:ts-medical]].

::: note Changed in 2026
From 15 January 2026, people with ADHD or autism no longer have to send a doctor's certificate when they apply [[src:ts-medical]].
:::

## The personal requirements

You must also be suitable as a driver "with regard to your personal circumstances". Transportstyrelsen may collect information from other authorities, for example the police.

::: law kkl 3:2
> Lämplighet med hänsyn till de personliga förhållandena förutsätter att sökanden inte är opålitlig i nykterhetshänseende och i övrigt kan antas komma att respektera trafikreglerna och visa hänsyn, omdöme och ansvar i trafiken.

**In English:** "Being suitable with regard to personal circumstances requires that the applicant is not unreliable in matters of sobriety, and can otherwise be expected to respect the traffic rules and to show consideration, judgement and responsibility in traffic."
:::

If something in your personal circumstances stands in the way, the permit is refused and a [[term:sparrtid|waiting period]] is set, before which you cannot get a licence. That period is at least one month and at most three years [[src:kkl|3:9]].

## When the permit runs out

A permit is valid for [[fact:permit.validity]] and cannot be extended. If you want to keep practising or take a test after that, you must apply for a new one [[src:ts-permit]]. Plan ahead: the permit must also be valid on the day of each test.

::: only car-automatic
**Removing code 78 later.** If you get an automatic-only licence (code 78) and later want to drive manual cars, you do **not** need a new learner's permit for the manual driving test, as long as the automatic condition was not set for medical reasons [[src:kkl|3:7a]] [[src:ts-permit]]. Private practice for it still needs an approved supervisor. See [[lesson:code-78]].
:::

::: only motorcycle
**Motorcycle and car on the same permit.** The A licence is in group 1, like B. If you already study for a car licence, the same permit covers the motorcycle licence while it is valid.
:::

::: warning
Driving a car or motorcycle without being entitled to do so is the crime *olovlig körning* (unlawful driving) [[src:tbl|3]]. Practising privately with someone who is not an approved supervisor counts as unlawful driving too. Besides a fine, your learner's permit can be revoked [[src:ts-supervisor]].
:::
