## Booking and price

You book the theory test in **Trafikverket's booking service**. You cannot book without a valid learner's permit [[src:tv-theory-b]]. A theory test costs **[[fact:prices.theory]]** on weekdays during the day and **[[fact:prices.theoryEvening]]** after 18:00 and at weekends; the photo for your licence costs **[[fact:prices.photo]]** [[src:tv-prices]].

- You can cancel or move a test free of charge up to **24 hours** before it starts. Later than that, or if you do not turn up, you still pay [[src:tv-booking]].
- If you are **under 18** when you book, the test must be paid at least **25 hours** before it starts, or it is cancelled automatically [[src:tv-booking]].
- You may only take **[[fact:theory.perDay]] test per day** for the same category [[src:tv-theory-b]].

::: tip
Trafikverket's advice: book the theory test and the driving test at the same time, a few weeks apart, so you have time for a new theory test before the driving test if needed [[src:tv-theory-b]].
:::

## On the day

::: steps
1. **Arrive at least [[fact:theory.arriveEarly]] early.** You are photographed before your first theory test, and that photo goes on your licence [[src:tv-before]].
2. **Show a valid physical ID document.** Accepted: a SIS-marked company, service or ID card; a Swedish national ID card; a national ID card from another EU country issued on 2 August 2021 or later; an ID card from Skatteverket; a Swedish driving licence; a Swedish or other EU passport; or a passport from the Faroe Islands, the United Kingdom, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland. Digital IDs (Freja eID, the digital BankID card) are **not** accepted [[src:tv-before]].
3. **Both parts of the risk education** must be done and valid [[src:tv-theory-b]].
4. **Turn your phone off completely.** Everything you need is in the test room [[src:tv-theory-b]].
5. You watch a short film about the test, and then you start the test yourself on a touch screen. The time starts when you start [[src:tv-theory-b]].
:::

## The format

::: key The theory test in numbers
- **[[fact:theory.questions]] questions**, each with answer options and **one** correct answer.
- **[[fact:theory.minutes]] minutes.**
- **[[fact:theory.scored]] points** at most, because **5 questions are trial questions** that do not count.
- **Pass: [[fact:theory.passMark]] points or more.**
:::

This is the same for the car test and the motorcycle test [[src:tv-theory-b]] [[src:tv-mc]]. At your seat there are **headphones** so you can listen to the questions in Swedish, and a **calculator**. You may raise your hand to ask about a word or a synonym [[src:tv-theory-b]].

When you finish you see at once whether you passed. You are told which **areas** you made mistakes in, but not which questions. Trafikverket keeps the questions secret because they are reused [[src:tv-theory-b]].

## The five areas

Trafikverket says the test measures knowledge in five areas [[src:tv-theory-b]]:

| Area | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Vehicle knowledge and handling** (*Fordonskännedom och manövrering*) | How the vehicle works, how the road surface affects it, risks when something is wrong with the vehicle, and how your driving affects how it behaves. |
| **Environment** (*Miljö*) | How choices of transport affect the environment, and how to drive safely and with less environmental impact. |
| **Traffic safety** (*Trafiksäkerhet*) | Good judgement with other road users, adapting speed and position, and how road conditions and surroundings affect drivers. |
| **Traffic rules** (*Trafikregler*) | Road signs, road markings, speed, meeting and overtaking, load, passengers and duties towards other road users. |
| **Personal factors** (*Personliga förutsättningar*) | Group pressure, deliberate or impulsive actions, and how unwillingness to follow rules affects safety. |

Transportstyrelsen's **course plans** (*kursplaner*) set out the content and goals of the driver education for each category – TSFS 2011:20 for cars and TSFS 2011:19 for motorcycles [[src:ts-curricula]]. The lessons on this site follow them and cite them wherever they set a requirement.

::: warning Do not just memorise answers
The questions you practise on the internet or get from a driving school are **not** the questions in the real test. Trafikverket stresses that you need broad knowledge of the whole subject rather than answers to single questions [[src:tv-theory-b]]. That is also true of the practice questions on this site.
:::

## Languages and support

When you book, you choose the language. The category B test is translated into **[[fact:theory.languages]] languages**, including **English (British English)**. You can also switch to Swedish during the test [[src:tv-languages]]. The other languages are Albanian, Arabic, Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian, Finnish, French, Persian, Russian, Somali, Sorani, Spanish, Thai, Tigrinya, Turkish and German [[src:tv-languages]].

If you cannot read and understand any of these, you can apply to take the test **with an interpreter**. Then you sit with the examiner and the interpreter in a separate room, and the test time for car and motorcycle is [[fact:theory.interpreterMinutes]] minutes [[src:tv-languages]].

If you have reading difficulties, dyslexia, ADHD, autism, a language disorder or another reason to need support, you can apply for **extended time or an oral test** [[src:tv-theory-b]].

## How long a pass is valid

::: law tsfs-2026-47 5:1
> Ett godkänt kunskapsprov vid Trafikverket eller Försvarsmakten är giltigt i ett år från dagen för provet.

**In English:** "A passed theory test at Trafikverket or the Armed Forces is valid for one year from the day of the test."
:::

This rule applies to theory tests passed **on or after [[fact:theory.validityChangeDate]]**. You must pass the driving test within that year. A theory test passed before that date is valid for four months [[src:tv-theory-b]].

## Cheating

Before the test you promise not to use unauthorised aids. If you cheat, you are **reported to the police** for a false declaration, **banned from tests for one or two years**, your test is cancelled and declared invalid, and any other booked test is cancelled [[src:tv-general]].
