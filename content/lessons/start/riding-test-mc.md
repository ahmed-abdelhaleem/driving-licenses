## The basics

The riding test for category A costs **[[fact:prices.drivingA]]** on weekdays during the day and **[[fact:prices.drivingAEvening]]** after 18:00 and at weekends [[src:tv-prices]]. Expect it to take **most of the day** – your booking confirmation says what applies. **You are responsible for bringing the motorcycle**, and you use the same motorcycle for the whole test [[src:tv-mc]].

The test has three parts [[src:tv-mc]]:

1. a **safety check**,
2. **special manoeuvres** (the manoeuvre test), and
3. **riding in traffic**.

## 1. The safety check

The test starts with a check of a selection of these [[src:tv-mc]]:

- lights, direction indicators, reflectors and the horn,
- brakes,
- tyres and wheel rims,
- power transmission (chain, belt or shaft),
- the stand,
- mirrors,
- bearings (steering head, swingarm and wheel bearings),
- suspension and shock absorbers,
- engine oil level,
- the emergency stop (kill) switch, if there is one.

If you find a fault, you should be able to suggest what to do. [[lesson:mc-safety-check]] shows how.

## 2. The manoeuvre test

The manoeuvre test (*manöverprovet*) is on a closed area and has three parts [[src:tv-mc]].

### Low-speed part

You **push the motorcycle with the engine off** into the start position in an angled corridor. Then you **crawl** through a course with left and right turns, which includes **stopping and starting while turning**. The speed should mainly be controlled **with the clutch**; you may support with the throttle and the brake [[src:tv-mc]].

### High-speed part

You accelerate to **at least [[fact:drivingTest.mcEvasionSpeed]]**, make an **evasive manoeuvre** and ride a **serpentine (slalom)** at the same speed. At the end you slow down and **turn around the last cone**, accelerate back to about 50 km/h, ride the serpentine back, and finish with an **efficient, controlled braking to a stop using both the front and the rear brake** [[src:tv-mc]].

The examiner assesses that you can handle the controls, balance, crawl and steer, and use the right riding and steering technique in an emergency where you cannot stop before an obstacle [[src:tv-mc]].

### Braking

The manoeuvre test ends with efficient, controlled braking to a standstill from **[[fact:drivingTest.mcBrakeSpeeds]]**, using both brakes. The examiner looks at your riding position, braking technique and how quickly you stop [[src:tv-mc]]. [[lesson:mc-braking]] explains the technique.

## 3. Riding in traffic

You and the examiner talk by **radio**, provided by Trafikverket. You ride in and outside towns, to a given destination or along a given route, for **at least [[fact:drivingTest.mcTrafficMinutes]]** in total – if possible on roads with different surfaces, widths, curves and amounts of traffic [[src:tv-mc]]. At some places you may need to ride the motorcycle from the manoeuvre area to where the traffic part starts.

## The motorcycle you bring

At the test the motorcycle must [[src:tv-mc]]:

- have a **manual gearbox and a clutch lever** (unless the test is for an automatic-only licence),
- be **registered in Sweden** – show the current registration certificate. If the certificate does not state the engine power, get a certificate of the power from Transportstyrelsen and bring it,
- be **insured** and have its **tax paid**,
- be **roadworthy**, and
- not be an emergency vehicle or have a driving ban (*körförbud*).

::: key Requirements for a category A test motorcycle
- Can reach at least **100 km/h**.
- Net power of at least **50 kW**, and a kerb weight over **175 kg**.
- With a combustion engine: at least **595 cm³**.
- Electric: at least **0.25 kW per kg** [[src:tv-mc]].
:::

## Moving up from A1 or A2

If you already have a motorcycle licence, you need a valid learner's permit, a **new photo** at a Trafikverket licence office before the riding test, and a **passed riding test**. You only need the risk education again if you got A1 before 1 November 2009 [[src:tv-mc]].
