## Your licence card

A licence is valid from the day it is issued [[src:kkl|3:11]]. Transportstyrelsen sends licence cards by **registered mail (REK) to a PostNord service point** near your registered address. You must **collect it in person** and show a valid ID document – PostNord does not accept e-ID for registered mail. The card waits at the service point for **14 days** before it is returned [[src:ts-delivery]].

::: law kkl 3:15
> Den som kör ett fordon som avses i 2 kap. 1 § ska ha med sig 1. körkortet, eller 2. om körkortet efter utfärdande ännu inte har lämnats ut och färden äger rum inom två månader från och med den dag då det utfärdades, en handling som styrker förarens identitet.

**In English:** "Anyone driving a vehicle covered by chapter 2, section 1 [cars, motorcycles and more] must carry 1. the licence, or 2. if the licence has been issued but not yet handed out and the journey takes place within two months from the day it was issued, a document that proves the driver's identity."
:::

You must show the licence (or the ID document) if a police officer or vehicle inspector asks for it. A licence that has not been collected within **one year** from the day it was issued is not valid [[src:kkl|3:13]].

## Probation – the first two years

::: law kkl 3:18
> För ett körkort som utfärdats efter godkänt förarprov gäller en prövotid av ett år för behörigheten AM och två år för övriga behörigheter från dagen för provet.

**In English:** "A licence issued after a passed licence test has a probation period of one year for category AM and two years for other categories, counted from the day of the test."
:::

During probation the traffic rules are the same for you as for every other driver. The difference is what happens **if your licence is revoked**:

::: warning Revoked during probation means starting again
If you commit an offence during the probation period that leads to your licence being revoked – for example speeding, drink-driving or running a red light – you must apply for a **new learner's permit**, do the **risk education again**, and pass **both the theory test and the driving test again**. The new licence then gets a new probation period [[src:ts-revoked]] [[src:kkl|5:15]].
:::

For comparison: a driver who is **not** on probation and gets a waiting period of one year or less for, say, speeding or a red light can get a new licence without new tests. When the waiting period is longer than one year, everyone must take the tests again [[src:ts-revoked]].

If you already have a licence in another category (other than AM) when you pass, your new category only has the probation time that is left on the older licence [[src:kkl|3:18]].

## Renewing your licence

A licence for categories AM, A1, A2, A, B and BE must be **renewed within [[fact:licence.renewal]]** of being issued or last renewed, and also if it is lost, destroyed or a detail on it changes [[src:kkl|3:14]]. Renewal includes a new photo at Trafikverket.

## Reading your licence card

The back of the card lists your categories with symbols, the date each category was first issued, and in **field 12** any conditions in coded form – for example **78** for automatic only [[src:ts-card]].

## Changes coming from the EU

The EU has adopted a fourth driving licence directive. Most of its rules apply in Sweden from **26 November 2029**, and Sweden still has to decide how some of them will work [[src:ts-future]]. The changes that matter most for new drivers:

| Change | When |
|---|---|
| **Accompanied driving from 17.** A B licence from 17, but until 18 you may only drive with a companion in the front seat who is at least 24, has held an EU licence for five years and has not had it revoked in that time. | From 26 November 2028 |
| **Heavier alternative-fuel cars on a B licence.** After two years with B, you may drive cars up to 4,250 kg that run fully or partly on alternative fuels (for example electricity or biogas). Not motorhomes. | From 26 November 2027 |
| **More ways to remove code 78.** A special training course in a manual vehicle may replace the new driving test – Sweden has not decided whether to offer it. | Most rules from 26 November 2029 |
| **Digital driving licence** in an EU digital identity wallet; a physical card will still be possible. | At the latest 2031 |

::: note
These rules were **not yet in force** when this site was checked ([[fact:checkedOn]]). Learn and drive by today's rules.
:::
