## Three ways to practise

You can practise with a **driving school** (*trafikskola*), **privately** with an approved [[term:handledare|supervisor]], or with both. Most learners combine them. To practise you need [[src:ts-practice]]:

- a valid **learner's permit**,
- to meet the **age limit** for the vehicle,
- a **valid ID document** with you, and
- for private practice: a **supervisor approved by Transportstyrelsen**.

If you only practise with a driving school, the learner's permit is enough.

## Age limits

::: only car
For a car (category B) you may start practising [[src:kkl|4:2]] [[src:ts-practice]]:

| What you practise with | Lowest age |
|---|---|
| Passenger car without a trailer | 16 years |
| Passenger car with a light trailer | 17 years 6 months |
| Light truck (with or without a light trailer) | 17 years 6 months |
:::

::: only motorcycle
For a heavy motorcycle (category A) the age to start practising depends on your experience [[src:kkl|4:2]] [[src:kkl|4:3]] [[src:ts-a]]:

| Your situation | Lowest age |
|---|---|
| No A2 licence | 23 years |
| You have held an A2 licence for at least 1 year and 6 months | 19 years 6 months |
| Only at a driving school, with the instructor riding on the motorcycle or in a sidecar | 17 years 6 months |

A three-wheeled motorcycle for category A needs you to be 20 to practise [[src:ts-a]].
:::

## Who can be a supervisor

A supervisor (*handledare*) for private practice must be approved by Transportstyrelsen **for each learner**. To be approved, the supervisor must [[src:kkl|4:7]] [[src:ts-supervisor]]:

- be at least **[[fact:ages.supervisor]]** old,
- have held a licence for the vehicle type for a total of at least **[[fact:supervisor.experience]]**, and
- supervise a learner who has a valid learner's permit.

A person **cannot** be approved if, during the last three years, their licence was revoked for more than three months because of an important traffic rule (for example speeding or a red light), or was revoked at all because of drink-driving, unreliability about sobriety or a serious crime, or if they have had an alcohol interlock condition. A supervisor can have at most **[[fact:supervisor.maxLearners]] learners** at the same time [[src:ts-supervisor]].

The approval is valid for **[[fact:supervisor.validity]]**, and it ends at once if the supervisor's licence is revoked [[src:kkl|4:8]]. Applying is done in Transportstyrelsen's e-service, and it normally takes up to four weeks [[src:ts-supervisor]].

::: note Changed on 1 August 2026
Until 31 July 2026, both the supervisor and the learner had to take an introduction course (*introduktionsutbildning*) before private practice in a car. Since **[[fact:supervisor.introCourseRemoved]]** this course is no longer required. The other requirements remain: the supervisor must still apply and be approved, and the learner must still have a valid learner's permit [[src:ts-intro-removed]].
:::

## Rules on every private practice drive

::: law kkl 4:5
> Övningskörning ska ske under uppsikt av någon som har vana och skicklighet att köra fordon av det slag som övningskörningen avser …

**In English:** "Practice driving must take place under the supervision of someone with experience of and skill in driving vehicles of the kind the practice is for …"
:::

- **The supervisor counts as the driver** and is responsible for the drive [[src:ts-supervisor]].
- The supervisor must carry the **supervisor certificate** (*handledarbevis*) – on paper, or shown by logging in to a digital mailbox if the police ask [[src:ts-supervisor]].
- The learner must carry an **ID document** [[src:kkl|3:15a]].
- The green and white **ÖVNINGSKÖR** sign must be clearly visible from behind, without blocking the view [[src:ts-practice]].
- You may carry passengers, as long as the practice can be done safely [[src:ts-practice]].

::: only car
**Manual or automatic?** You may practise in both kinds of car, unless your learner's permit limits you to automatic cars. If you practise in a manual car, your supervisor may not have code 78 (automatic only) on their own licence [[src:ts-faq]].
:::

::: only motorcycle
**Supervising a motorcycle learner.** The supervisor must either ride as a passenger on the same motorcycle or ride their own motorcycle – not follow in a car. A supervisor can only practise with **one learner at a time**. The ÖVNINGSKÖR sign may be printed on your clothing, but it must be clearly visible from behind [[src:ts-practice]].
:::

::: warning Paid private lessons
If someone charges money for private practice, it can count as running a driving school, which needs a permit from Transportstyrelsen. Practising privately with someone who is not an approved supervisor counts as unlawful driving, and can lead to your learner's permit being revoked [[src:ts-supervisor]].
:::

## Practice abroad

Each country has its own rules for practice driving. The Swedish rules only apply in Sweden, so check the rules of the country first [[src:ts-practice]].
