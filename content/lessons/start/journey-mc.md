## Three motorcycle categories

There are three licence categories for two-wheeled motorcycles [[src:ts-mc]] [[src:kkl|2:5]]:

| Category | Swedish name | What it covers | Lowest age for the licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | lätt motorcykel | At most 125 cm³ and 11 kW, and at most 0.1 kW per kg of kerb weight | 16 |
| **A2** | mellanstor motorcykel | At most 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg; a restricted motorcycle may not come from one with more than 70 kW | 18 |
| **A** | tung motorcykel | **All** two- and three-wheeled motorcycles, whatever the size or power | 24, or 20 after two years with A2 |

This course is for **category A**. A heavy three-wheeled motorcycle (over 15 kW) needs you to be at least 21 [[src:ts-vehicles]].

## Two ways to category A

::: law kkl 3:1
> … har fyllt … 20 år och har haft körkort med behörigheten A2 under minst två år, eller har fyllt 24 år, för behörigheten A …

**In English:** "… has reached … the age of 20 and has held a licence with category A2 for at least two years, or has reached the age of 24, for category A …"
:::

- **Direct access:** you are at least **24**. You take the theory test and the riding test for A.
- **Step access:** you have held **A2 for at least two years** and are at least **20**. You only take a **riding test** – no new theory test is needed if you already have A1 or A2 [[src:kkl|3:4]] [[src:ts-a]].

## What you need

Transportstyrelsen lists these requirements for an A licence [[src:ts-a]]:

1. A **learner's permit**.
2. You **live permanently in Sweden**, or have **studied here for at least six months**.
3. The **age** above.
4. A valid **risk education for motorcycles** – or an A1 or A2 licence for which you did the motorcycle risk education. If you took A1 before 1 November 2009, you must do the risk education.
5. A **passed licence test** (theory test and riding test). With A1 or A2 already, only the riding test.

You cannot hold a licence from another EEA country; it can be exchanged for a Swedish one instead [[src:ts-a]].

## The steps, in order

::: steps
1. **Apply for a learner's permit** (group 1). See [[lesson:learner-permit]].
2. **Practise riding** – from 23, or 19 years 6 months with 18 months of A2, or 17 years 6 months at a driving school. See [[lesson:practice-driving]].
3. **Do the motorcycle risk education**, parts 1 and 2. The car course does not count. See [[lesson:risk-education]].
4. **Pass the theory test.** See [[lesson:theory-test]].
5. **Pass the riding test** with its manoeuvre track and traffic riding, on your own motorcycle. See [[lesson:riding-test-mc]].
6. **Collect your licence and ride on probation** for the first [[fact:probation.length]]. See [[lesson:after-the-test]].
:::

## What an A licence also covers

An A licence also lets you drive class I and class II mopeds, class a tractors and class II motor implements [[src:ts-vehicles]]. It does **not** include cars – for that you need category B.

::: tip Car licence and motorcycle licence
Many riders already have a B licence. The traffic rules and signs are the same, but the motorcycle theory test is a separate test with questions written for riders, and the risk education must be the motorcycle version.
:::
