## The basics

After the theory test comes the driving test (*körprov*). It costs **[[fact:prices.drivingB]]** on weekdays during the day and **[[fact:prices.drivingBEvening]]** after 18:00 and at weekends. You must use a car from Trafikverket or from a driving school – **not your own car**. Hiring Trafikverket's car costs **[[fact:prices.carHire]]** [[src:tv-prices]] [[src:tv-driving-b]].

**[[fact:drivingTest.minutesB]]** are set aside for the test. You drive both in town traffic and on country roads [[src:tv-driving-b]].

::: only car-automatic
If the test car has an automatic gearbox, your licence gets **code 78** (automatic only). See [[lesson:code-78]].
:::

The test has three parts [[src:tv-driving-b]]:

1. **a safety check** of the car,
2. **special manoeuvres**, and
3. **driving in traffic**.

## 1. The safety check

The examiner asks you to check some of these [[src:tv-driving-b]]:

- lights, direction indicators, reflectors and the horn,
- windscreen wipers and washers,
- steering,
- brakes,
- tyres and wheel rims,
- fluids (for example washer fluid, coolant and engine oil),
- warning systems.

If you find a fault, you should be able to **suggest what to do about it**. If you cannot do part of the check yourself, ask the examiner for help. When you get in, adjust the **seat, head restraint, steering wheel, mirrors and seat belt** [[src:tv-driving-b]]. The [[lesson:safety-check-car]] lesson shows how to do each check.

## 2. Special manoeuvres

During the test the examiner may ask you to [[src:tv-driving-b]]:

- **park**, for example in a parking space or a parking bay along the road,
- **reverse** – the examiner says clearly where and how far,
- **start on a hill**, uphill or downhill,
- **turn the car around**, at a place given to you or one you choose,
- **start from the roadside**,
- use a **control** while driving, for example switching on the wipers when it starts to rain, and
- do an **efficient braking**: stop the car in the shortest possible distance from at least **[[fact:drivingTest.brakeSpeedB]]**. The examiner decides the speed and tells you when the exercise is over.

You may use technical aids in the car, such as a reversing camera, but only as a complement to looking around yourself [[src:tv-driving-b]].

## 3. Driving in traffic

You drive in town and on country roads. The examiner gives directions in good time and looks at [[src:tv-driving-b]]:

- **how you adapt your speed,**
- **your position on the road,** and
- **your attention and awareness of risks.**

You will also **drive on your own to a destination** – one you know, or one you follow signs to. If you take a wrong turn, you can turn around and continue; the examiner can also ask you to [[src:tv-driving-b]].

Situations that may come up include junctions with and without traffic lights, roundabouts, changing lanes, passing parked cars or a bus at a stop, unprotected road users and playing children, road works, railway or tram crossings, narrow and winding roads, a motorway or expressway with up to 120 km/h, joining and leaving country roads, overtaking, meeting traffic, darkness and poor visibility, and slippery roads [[src:tv-driving-b]]. The examiner may also ask questions linked to situations during the drive.

## What mistakes are allowed?

Trafikverket's answers to common questions [[src:tv-driving-b]]:

- **Small mistakes** that do not matter for safety are allowed, as long as they are not repeated too often. If you forget to indicate when leaving a roundabout, say so and remember next time.
- **Stalling the engine** once can be fine if it does not cause danger. If it happens many times, you need more practice.
- **Slowing down too much or using too low a gear** in a roundabout can lead to a fail, because it can show poor planning.

::: warning Things that stop the test before it starts
The most common reason for not being allowed to take the test is an invalid ID document – for example one that has expired [[src:tv-driving-b]]. Filming or recording sound during the test is not allowed [[src:tv-general]].
:::

::: tip Nervous?
That is normal. Tell the examiner – you can talk for a moment before the test starts [[src:tv-driving-b]].
:::
