## What a B licence lets you drive

A licence with category B (*behörighet B*) is the normal car licence. According to the Driving Licence Act and Transportstyrelsen, it covers [[src:kkl|2:5]] [[src:ts-vehicles]]:

- **Passenger cars and light trucks** with a [[term:totalvikt|total weight]] of at most **[[fact:licence.maxWeightB]]**.
- **One light trailer** behind such a car. A trailer counts as light if its total weight is at most 750 kg, *or* if the car and the trailer together have a total weight of at most 3,500 kg.
- **Three-wheeled and four-wheeled motorcycles.** For a heavy three-wheeled motorcycle (over 15 kW) you must be at least 21 [[src:kkl|2:6]].
- **Mopeds** of class I and class II. Every licence category includes category AM (class I mopeds), and any driving licence allows class II mopeds [[src:kkl|2:7b]] [[src:kkl|2:3]].
- Tractors, class I motor implements and terrain vehicles (*terrängvagn*) [[src:kkl|2:2]].

::: tip Heavier trailers
If you want to tow a heavier trailer, there are two extensions. **B96** (*utökad B*) allows a car and trailer with a combined total weight over 3,500 kg but not over [[fact:licence.b96Max]]; it needs a special driving test [[src:kkl|2:7a]]. **BE** is a separate category for trailers up to 3,500 kg [[src:ts-vehicles]]. Neither is part of this course.
:::

::: example Working out the trailer rule
Your car has a total weight of 1,900 kg. You can tow a trailer with a total weight of 750 kg (it is light whatever the car weighs). You can also tow a trailer with a total weight of 1,600 kg, because 1,900 + 1,600 = 3,500 kg. A trailer with a total weight of 1,700 kg would need B96, because 1,900 + 1,700 = 3,600 kg.
:::

The weights that count are the **total weights** written in the registration certificate, not what the car and trailer actually weigh today. [[lesson:towing-trailers]] explains this in detail.

## What you need to get a B licence

Transportstyrelsen lists these requirements [[src:ts-b]]:

1. A valid **learner's permit** (*körkortstillstånd*).
2. You **live permanently in Sweden**, or have **studied here for at least six months**.
3. You are at least **[[fact:ages.licenceB]]** old.
4. A valid **risk education** for passenger cars (both parts).
5. A **passed licence test** – both the theory test (*kunskapsprov*) and the driving test (*körprov*).

You also cannot hold a driving licence from another EEA country. If you do, you can exchange it for a Swedish one instead [[src:ts-b]].

## The steps, in order

::: steps
1. **Apply for a learner's permit** from Transportstyrelsen. See [[lesson:learner-permit]].
2. **Practise driving** – from the age of [[fact:ages.practiceCar]] – at a driving school, privately with an approved supervisor, or both. See [[lesson:practice-driving]].
3. **Do the risk education**, parts 1 and 2. Both parts must be done and valid before the theory test. See [[lesson:risk-education]].
4. **Pass the theory test** at Trafikverket. See [[lesson:theory-test]].
5. **Pass the driving test** at Trafikverket. See [[lesson:driving-test-car]].
6. **Collect your licence and drive on probation** for the first [[fact:probation.length]]. See [[lesson:after-the-test]].
:::

::: tip Book both tests together
Trafikverket advises booking the theory test and the driving test at the same time, with a few weeks between them. Then you have time to take the theory test again before the driving test if you need to [[src:tv-theory-b]].
:::

## Manual or automatic?

The theory test is **the same** for manual and automatic cars. The only difference is the car you use in the driving test. If you take the driving test in a car with an automatic gearbox (no clutch pedal), your licence gets **condition code 78**, which means you may only drive cars with automatic transmission [[src:tv-theory-b]]. A licence from a test in a manual car lets you drive both.

::: only car-automatic
You are on the automatic path. [[lesson:code-78]] explains code 78, why many learners choose it, and how to remove it later if you want to.
:::

::: only car-manual
You are on the manual path. The [[lesson:manual-gearbox]] lesson teaches the clutch and gears for both the theory and the practical test.
:::
