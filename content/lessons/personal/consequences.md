## Two separate consequences

When you break a traffic rule, two different things can happen:

1. **A penalty** – a fine or prison. Decided by the police, a prosecutor or a court.
2. **A licence decision** (*körkortsingripande*) – your licence is **revoked**, you get a **warning**, or (after drink-driving) an **alcolock condition**. Decided by **Transportstyrelsen** [[src:kkl|5:1]].

The licence decision is based on the final judgment, an accepted fine or an accepted on-the-spot fine [[src:kkl|5:1]]. Both can follow from the same offence – Sweden's Supreme Administrative Court has found that this is **not** a second punishment for the same act [[src:ts-police-seize]].

## Fines for breaking the traffic rules

A driver of a motor vehicle who breaks many of the rules in the Road Traffic Ordinance – for example the **speed limits** – is fined [[src:tf|14:3]]. For many offences the police give an **on-the-spot fine** (*ordningsbot*). If you accept and sign it, the penalty part is settled [[src:ts-police-seize]] – but Transportstyrelsen may still act on your licence.

## Traffic crimes

| Crime | What it is | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| **Careless driving** (*vårdslöshet i trafik*) | Seriously failing to take the care and caution that the situation requires to avoid an accident | Day-fines [[src:tbl|1]] |
| **Gross careless driving** (*grov vårdslöshet i trafik*) | **Gross** negligence or **obvious indifference** to other people's lives or property, when driving a motor vehicle | Prison up to **2 years** [[src:tbl|1]] |
| **Unlawful driving** ([[term:olovlig-korning|olovlig körning]]) | Knowingly driving a vehicle that needs a licence **without the right to drive it** – for example without a licence, during a ban, or without the right category | Fines – up to **6 months** in prison if your licence had been revoked, it is a habit, or the offence is otherwise grave [[src:tbl|3]] |
| **Letting someone drive unlawfully** | Allowing a person who has no right to drive a vehicle to drive it | Fines [[src:tbl|3]] |
| **Drink- and drug-driving** | See [[lesson:alcohol-and-drugs]] | Fines or prison up to 6 months; aggravated: up to 2 years [[src:tbl|4]] [[src:tbl|4a]] |
| **Leaving the scene of an accident** ([[term:smitning|smitning]]) | Leaving an accident you were involved in without helping or giving your name and information | Fines or prison up to **6 months**; up to **1 year** if grave [[src:tbl|5]] |

A vehicle used in a traffic crime can be **confiscated** if it is needed to prevent more crimes – especially after repeated unlawful driving or drink-driving [[src:tbl|7]].

## When the police take your licence

The police **must** take your licence on the spot if, for example [[src:kkl|5:7]] [[src:ts-police-seize]]:

- you show **clear signs** of being affected by alcohol or another substance while driving,
- it is likely that the licence will be **revoked** – for example for **drink-driving**, **gross careless driving**, **speeding**, **running a red light**, not stopping at a **stop sign** or keeping **too short a distance**.

::: steps
1. The police send your licence and their decision to **Transportstyrelsen** within **48 hours** [[src:ts-police-seize]].
2. Transportstyrelsen investigates and sends you a letter; you have **14 days** to give your side [[src:ts-police-seize]].
3. Transportstyrelsen decides – revocation with a waiting period, a warning, or your licence back. The waiting period counts **from the day the police took your licence** [[src:ts-police-seize]].
:::

::: warning The 48 hours
For offences such as speeding, the police may let you **drive for 48 hours** after taking your licence [[src:kkl|5:7]]. After that, all driving is **unlawful driving** – even if Transportstyrelsen has not decided yet. The 48 hours cannot be extended [[src:ts-police-seize]].
:::

## Revocation and waiting periods

When your licence is **revoked** ([[term:aterkallelse|återkallelse]]), Transportstyrelsen sets a **waiting period** ([[term:sparrtid|spärrtid]]) – the time during which you may not get a new licence. It is **at least 1 month and at most 3 years**, and at least **1 year** for a grave crime such as aggravated drink-driving [[src:kkl|5:6]]. Transportstyrelsen judges each case, also considering earlier decisions and, for example, whether you need the licence for work [[src:ts-revocation]].

Typical waiting periods in Transportstyrelsen's practice [[src:ts-revocation]]:

| Offence | Usual waiting period |
|---|---|
| **Speeding 31–40 km/h** over the limit – the most common case | **2 months** |
| Speeding **21–30 km/h** over the limit where the limit is **30 km/h or lower** | **2 months** |
| More serious speeding | **Longer** |
| Driving against a **red light** | **2 months** |
| **Careless driving** | **4 months** is common |
| **Leaving the scene** of an accident | **2–6 months** |
| **Repeated** smaller offences within two years – for example minor speeding or not wearing a **seat belt** | **2 months** (or a warning if you have no earlier warning or revocation) |
| **Drug-driving** | **12 months** |
| **Drink-driving** with alcohol | At least **12 months** (24 for aggravated or repeated) – or an alcolock |

Other grounds for revocation include **overtaking at a pedestrian crossing**, **unlawful driving**, failure to keep a safe **distance** [[src:ts-revocation]], and **medical** reasons such as an illness that makes driving unsafe [[src:kkl|5:3]].

## A warning instead

In less serious cases, Transportstyrelsen can give a **warning** ([[term:varning|varning]]) instead of revoking the licence [[src:kkl|5:9]]. You keep your licence – but the warning counts against you for **2 years** (**5 years** for drink-driving), and if you offend again the risk is high that the licence is revoked [[src:ts-warning]].

## Probation – the first two years

Your licence has a **probation period** ([[term:provotid|prövotid]]) of **two years** from the day of the test [[src:kkl|3:18]]. If your licence is **revoked** during probation – even for a waiting period of only two months – you must get a new **learner's permit**, do the **risk education** again and pass **both the theory test and the driving test** again. The new licence gets a **new probation period** [[src:ts-revoked]]. See [[lesson:after-the-test]].

## Getting a licence back

| Situation | What you need |
|---|---|
| **Not on probation**, waiting period **1 year or less**, for example for speeding or a red light | A new licence card is issued without a new test – you send a photo and signature and pay the fee [[src:ts-revoked]] |
| **Not on probation**, waiting period **1 year or less**, after drink-driving, gross careless driving, unreliability as to sobriety or another serious crime | A new **learner's permit** first [[src:ts-revoked]] [[src:kkl|5:14]] |
| Waiting period **longer than 1 year** | Learner's permit, **risk education** again, and **both tests** again [[src:ts-revoked]] |
| Revoked **during probation** | Learner's permit, risk education again and **both tests** again – whatever the waiting period [[src:ts-revoked]] |

::: key Is it worth it?
Speeding by 31 km/h on an ordinary road can mean **two months without a licence** – and during probation, **starting all over again** with permit, risk education and both tests. Keep to the limits.
:::
