## Why tyres matter so much

The tyres are the car's **only contact with the road**. Every movement – accelerating, braking, steering – depends on the **grip** ([[term:vaggrepp|sv]]) between a few small patches of rubber and the road. Worn tyres, the wrong tyres for the season or the wrong pressure can make the braking distance far longer and the car hard to control.

## Tread depth

::: law tsfs-2009-19 4
> Slitbana på däck får inte vara mer nött än att det kvarvarande profildjupet i huvudmönstret uppgår till 1,6 mm i de mittersta 75 % av slitbanans bredd.

**In English:** "The tread of a tyre may not be worn so much that the remaining depth of the main pattern is less than 1.6 mm in the middle 75 % of the tread width."
:::

| When | Minimum [[term:monsterdjup|tread depth]] for cars and light trucks |
|---|---|
| All year (summer conditions) | **[[fact:tyres.summerTread]]** [[src:tsfs-2009-19|4]] |
| **1 December – 31 March when there are winter road conditions** | **[[fact:tyres.winterTread]]** [[src:tsfs-2009-19|5]] |

These are **minimums**. Grip on wet roads, snow and ice gets much worse long before a tyre reaches the legal limit. A tyre may also not have cracks, holes or other damage that exposes its inner reinforcement [[src:tsfs-2009-19|6]].

## Winter tyres

::: law tf 4:18a
> Vid färd på väg under perioden den 1 december-31 mars ska följande fordon vara försedda med vinterdäck eller likvärdig utrustning när vinterväglag råder: 1. personbil klass I, 2. personbil klass II, 3. traktor som utgörs av en ombyggd bil, 4. lätt lastbil, 5. lätt buss, och 6. släpvagn som dras av ett fordon som avses i 1-5.

**In English:** "When driving on a road from 1 December to 31 March, the following vehicles must have winter tyres or equivalent equipment when there are winter road conditions: 1. class I passenger cars, 2. class II passenger cars, 3. tractors made from converted cars, 4. light trucks, 5. light buses, and 6. trailers towed by any of these."
:::

- **Winter road conditions** ([[term:vinterväglag|sv]]) means snow, ice, slush or frost on **any part** of the road [[src:tsfs-2009-19|3]] [[src:ts-winter-tyres]]. The **police** decide whether winter road conditions apply at a place [[src:ts-winter-tyres]].
- **Equivalent equipment** can be, for example, **snow chains** [[src:tsfs-2009-19|3]].
- The requirement does not apply to vehicles that are **30 years old or more** (model year) [[src:tf|4:18c]].

### What counts as a winter tyre?

A winter tyre for a car must be [[src:tsfs-2009-19|3]] [[src:ts-winter-tyres]]:

- marked with the **alpine symbol** – a mountain with three peaks and a snowflake (3PMSF),
- marked **POR** (*Professional Off Road* – a marking used on some tyres for commercial vehicles), **or**
- **studded** (and meet the stud rules).

Non-studded winter tyres may also have an **ice-grip marking**, showing that they passed a test on ice. That marking is not required [[src:ts-winter-tyres]]. A tyre marked only **M+S** ("mud and snow") does **not** count as a winter tyre on a car.

## Studded tyres

- Allowed from **1 October to 15 April**, and outside that period only when there are – or may be – winter road conditions [[src:tsfs-2009-19|8]] [[src:ts-winter-tyres]].
- A car may **not** use **both** studded and non-studded tyres at the same time [[src:tsfs-2009-19|10]].
- If the car has studded tyres and there are winter road conditions, a **trailer** it tows must also have studded tyres [[src:tsfs-2009-19|11]].
- Some streets ban studded tyres – sign [[sign:C44|C44]].

## Pressure

Wrong tyre pressure affects **handling**, **fuel consumption** and the **life** of the tyre [[src:ts-tyres]]. Find the right pressure in the owner's manual [[src:ts-tyres]], and check it often – with the tyres **cold**. Increase it as the manual says when the car is fully loaded.

## The best tyres at the back

::: key Best grip behind
Transportstyrelsen's advice: the tyres with the **deepest tread** or the best grip should be fitted on the **rear** axle – also on front-wheel-drive cars. That reduces the risk of a **skid** when braking and in bends. With studded tyres, the ones with the **most stud protrusion** go at the back [[src:ts-tyres]] [[src:tsfs-2009-19|10]].
:::

Why the rear? If the rear tyres lose grip first, the back of the car swings out (oversteer) – much harder to control than the front sliding. See [[lesson:skids-and-traction]].

## Storing tyres

Store tyres **cool, dry and dark** [[src:ts-tyres]].
