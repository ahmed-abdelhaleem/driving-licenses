## Why gear matters

A rider has no car body around them. Trafikverket points out that **even at legal speeds** the consequences of a motorcycle crash can be severe, and lists **helmet and other protective equipment** among the key safety measures [[src:tv-mc-safety]]. The curriculum requires you to explain the importance of personal protective equipment and to **use** it [[src:tsfs-2011-19|2:2]].

## The helmet – the law

::: law tf 4:9
> Skyddshjälm ska användas av den som färdas med 1. motorcykel, 2. moped, 3. traktor b utan karosseri, eller 4. terrängskoter utan karosseri.

**In English:** "A protective helmet must be worn by anyone travelling on 1. a motorcycle, 2. a moped, 3. a class b tractor without a body, or 4. a snowmobile or other off-road scooter without a body."
:::

- This applies to the **rider and the passenger** [[src:tf|4:9]].
- Exceptions include a **passenger under seven** who uses another suitable head protection, when the vehicle is **not moving**, and riding inside a **car park, petrol station or workshop area** [[src:tf|4:9]].
- The rider must make sure that passengers **under 15** wear a helmet [[src:tf|4:9]].

::: law tsfs-2017-94 3
> Vid färd med motorcykel eller moped ska en skyddshjälm användas som är E-märkt och typgodkänd enligt UN ECE-reglemente nummer 22 ändringsserie 05 eller senare ändringsserie.

**In English:** "When riding a motorcycle or moped, a protective helmet must be used that is E-marked and type-approved under UN ECE Regulation No. 22, amendment series 05 or a later series."
:::

So look for an **E label** in the helmet (often on the chin strap) showing approval to **ECE 22.05 or 22.06**.

::: key Wearing it correctly
- The helmet must be **securely fastened** so it sits firmly on the head [[src:tsfs-2017-94|5]].
- A helmet may not be used if something has been done to it that can reduce its protection [[src:tsfs-2017-94|6]] – Transportstyrelsen gives as examples **stickers, protruding objects or covers, holes in the shell, and paint** [[src:tsfs-2017-94|6]].
- A helmet that has taken a hard blow can be damaged inside even if it looks fine. Replace it.
- It should fit snugly: when fastened, it should not be possible to roll it off forwards or backwards.
:::

**Visor:** check before each ride that it is clean and in good condition – a scratched visor can limit your view, particularly in bright sunshine and in darkness [[src:uk-hc-mc|rule 83]] [[src:uk-hc-mc|rule 84]]. A **tinted** visor reduces what you see in poor light; use a clear one in darkness and dusk.

## Clothing and protectors

There is no law on clothing, but the right gear can prevent or reduce many injuries:

| Gear | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Jacket and trousers** | Abrasion-resistant leather or motorcycle textile, with **protectors** at shoulders, elbows, hips and knees. Should fit closely so protectors stay in place. |
| **Back protector** | Built into the jacket or worn separately. |
| **Gloves** | Real motorcycle gloves that cover the wrist, with reinforced palms – hands go out first in a fall. |
| **Boots** | Sturdy motorcycle boots that protect the ankle. Ordinary shoes can come off in a crash. |
| **Airbag vest or jacket** | Inflates in a crash to protect neck, back and chest. |

::: tip Be seen
Car drivers often fail to notice motorcycles – the curriculum names the risks of other road users having **difficulty seeing riders** [[src:tsfs-2011-19|3:2]]. **Bright colours and reflective material** on the jacket and helmet help – reflective material shows up in other vehicles' headlights from a longer distance in the dark [[src:uk-hc-mc|rule 86]] [[src:uk-hc-mc|rule 87]]. See [[lesson:mc-visibility-and-position]].
:::

## Weather and comfort

Being cold, wet or tired reduces your concentration and reaction speed. Rain gear, warm layers and a neck protector are safety equipment too. In summer, choose ventilated gear rather than riding in a T-shirt.

## The passenger

The passenger needs the same protection: an approved helmet, protective clothing, gloves and boots. Make sure the passenger's feet reach the **footrests**.
