## Why check the car?

The curriculum says you must be able to carry out **simple checks** and **suggest measures** so that the car is roadworthy [[src:tsfs-2011-20|2:2]]. The driving test starts with a **safety check** (*säkerhetskontroll*). The examiner tells you what to check, and you may be asked, for example, about [[src:tv-driving-b]]:

- lights, direction indicators, reflectors and horn,
- windscreen wipers and washers,
- steering,
- brakes,
- tyres and wheel rims,
- fluids – for example washer fluid, coolant and engine oil,
- warning systems.

If you find a fault, you must be able to **suggest what to do**. If there is a part you cannot do alone, ask the examiner for help [[src:tv-driving-b]].

## Lights, indicators, reflectors and horn

::: steps
1. Switch on the **ignition** and the lights.
2. Walk round the car: **parking lights**, **dipped beam**, **main beam**, **rear lights**, **number-plate light**, **indicators** (front, side, rear) and **hazard lights**.
3. **Brake lights** and **reversing light**: ask someone to look, or look at the reflection in a window or garage door.
4. **Reflectors**: whole and clean.
5. Press the **horn**.
:::

**Fault?** A broken bulb or lamp must be replaced before you drive in conditions where it is needed. Dirty or snow-covered lamps must be cleaned.

## Windscreen, wipers and washers

Check that the wipers clean the glass without streaks, the rubber blades are whole, and the washer works with enough washer fluid – in winter fluid that does not freeze. A windscreen with cracks or damage in the driver's field of view should be repaired.

## Steering

With the engine running and the car standing still, turn the wheel gently. The wheels should start to turn almost **at once** – a lot of **free play** (*glapp*) before the wheels react is a fault. On the road, the car should go straight without pulling to one side. Power steering should feel even.

**Fault?** Steering faults are dangerous – do not drive; have the car repaired.

## Brakes

- **Foot brake:** with the engine running, press the pedal. It should stop **well above the floor** and feel **firm**, not spongy, and not sink slowly under steady pressure.
- **Brake fluid:** the level in the reservoir between the **MIN and MAX** marks.
- **Parking brake:** it should hold the car on a slope.
- **Test on the move:** at low speed where it is safe, brake gently – the car should stop straight without pulling or noises.
- A **red brake warning light** that stays on means stop and check.

**Fault?** A soft pedal, falling fluid level or a red brake light: do not drive – contact a workshop.

## Tyres and rims

- **Tread depth** – at least **1.6 mm** in summer conditions, and at least **3 mm** on winter tyres when they are required [[src:ts-tyres]]. Many tyres have small **tread wear indicators** in the grooves.
- **Damage** – cuts, bulges, objects stuck in the tyre, damaged rims.
- **Air pressure** – check when the tyres are **cold**, against the values in the owner's manual [[src:ts-tyres]] (many cars also have them on a label in the door frame or the fuel flap).
- **Wheel bolts** – none missing.

Tyres are explained in detail in [[lesson:tyres]].

## Fluids under the bonnet

| Fluid | How to check | If low |
|---|---|---|
| **Engine oil** | On level ground, engine off and preferably cold. Pull the **dipstick**, wipe it, push it in fully, pull it out again: the level should be between the **MIN and MAX** marks. Some cars show it on the dashboard instead. | Top up with the oil type in the owner's manual. A **red oil-pressure light** while driving: stop at once. |
| **Coolant** | The level in the expansion tank between **MIN and MAX**, when the engine is cold. **Never open the cap on a hot engine** – the hot liquid is under pressure. | Top up with the right coolant. Keep it frost-proof in winter. |
| **Brake fluid** | The reservoir level between MIN and MAX. | A falling level can mean a leak or worn brake pads – go to a workshop. |
| **Washer fluid** | The tank level. | Top up – with antifreeze washer fluid in winter. |

## Warning systems

Switch on the ignition: most warning lights come on for a moment as a **self-test** and then go out. A light that **stays on** shows a fault. See the colour rules in [[lesson:car-controls]].

## Driving a car with faults

The owner must make sure the car is not used in breach of the rules [[src:tf|1:5]], and you may never drive in a way that is unsafe for the conditions [[src:tf|3:14]]. Faults in **brakes, steering, tyres** or **lights in darkness** make the car dangerous – do not drive it until it is fixed. A car with serious faults can also be given a **driving ban** (*körförbud*) at a roadside check or an inspection.
