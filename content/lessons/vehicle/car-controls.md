## Before you drive: set up your position

At the driving test you must adjust the **seat, head restraint, steering wheel, mirrors and seat belt** when you get in [[src:tv-driving-b]]. Do it in this order every time:

::: steps
1. **Seat distance** – you can press the pedals fully down with a slight bend left in the knee.
2. **Backrest** – your wrists reach the top of the steering wheel with your back against the backrest, so your arms are slightly bent when you hold the wheel.
3. **Seat height** – you can see well over the wheel and the instruments are visible.
4. **Head restraint** – its top roughly at the height of the top of your head, and close to the back of your head. It protects your neck in a rear-end collision (whiplash).
5. **Steering wheel** – if it can be adjusted, set it so you can see the instruments and reach comfortably.
6. **Mirrors** – the inside mirror shows the whole rear window; the door mirrors show a little of your own car's side and as much as possible of the lanes beside you.
7. **Seat belt** – lap part low over the hips, diagonal part over the middle of the shoulder, no twists, no slack.
:::

## The pedals

| Pedal | Manual car | Automatic car |
|---|---|---|
| Left | **Clutch** (left foot) | – (no clutch; often a wide brake pedal) |
| Middle | **Brake** (right foot) | – |
| Right | **Accelerator** (right foot) | **Accelerator** (right foot) |

In an automatic car there are only two pedals: **brake and accelerator, both with the right foot**. The left foot rests. See [[lesson:manual-gearbox]] or [[lesson:automatic-gearbox]].

## The main controls

- **Steering wheel** – hold it with both hands, roughly at the "quarter to three" position, so the airbag can deploy freely.
- **Indicator stalk** (usually left of the wheel) – signal before you turn, change lanes or pull out [[src:tf|3:65]].
- **Light switch** – dipped beam, main beam (often by pushing the stalk away from you), fog lights, parking lights. See [[lesson:vehicle-lights]].
- **Wipers and washers** (usually right of the wheel). At the test you may be asked to use them yourself, for example when it starts to rain [[src:tv-driving-b]].
- **Horn** – only to prevent danger [[src:tf|3:64]].
- **Hazard warning lights** – the red triangle button.
- **Parking brake** – a lever or an electric button. It holds the car still when parked and helps when you start on a hill.
- **Climate controls and demister** – clear windows are a safety matter. Use the **defroster** for the windscreen and the **rear window heating** when the windows mist up or freeze.

## Warning lights

The dashboard lights tell you about the car's condition. At the test you may be asked to check the car's **warning systems** [[src:tv-driving-b]]. Most cars use the same colour logic:

| Colour | Means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Red** | A serious fault or danger – for example **brake system**, **oil pressure**, **engine temperature**, **charging** or **seat belt not fastened** | **Stop as soon as it is safe** and find out why. Do not drive on with a red brake or oil warning. |
| **Yellow / orange** | A fault or something to check – for example **engine management**, **ABS**, **ESC**, **tyre pressure**, **low fuel** | Drive carefully and get it checked **soon**. Some systems may not work. |
| **Green / blue** | Information – indicators, dipped beam on, **main beam** (blue) | No action needed. |

::: warning Always read the owner's manual
Symbols differ between car makes. The owner's manual explains each one. When you borrow or rent a car, take a minute to learn its switches and lights **before** you drive off.
:::

## Mirrors and blind spots

The mirrors never show everything. The areas you cannot see in the mirrors are your **blind spots** ([[term:dodvinkel|sv]]) – mainly beside the rear corners of the car, but also behind the **windscreen pillars** (A-pillars), which can hide a pedestrian or cyclist at a junction.

::: key Mirror – signal – shoulder check
Before every change of position – pulling out, changing lanes, turning – check the mirrors, signal, and **turn your head** to look into the blind spot on that side. Move your head when you look past the windscreen pillars at junctions.
:::
